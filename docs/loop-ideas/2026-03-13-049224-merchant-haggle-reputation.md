# Merchant Haggle Reputation

**Category:** feature
**Priority:** medium
**Scope:** medium

## Description

Introduce a per-town "Merchant Reputation" that evolves based on a player's selling behavior in that town, turning the currently flat sell-item action into a strategic relationship-building system. Each town zone tracks a hidden reputation score (stored as a Redis key `merchant_rep:{playerId}:{zoneId}`) that shifts based on what and how the player sells: bulk-selling common junk in large batches earns "Reliable Supplier" reputation (+5% sell prices for materials), selling rare+ equipment earns "Connoisseur" reputation (+5% sell prices for equipment), while selling damaged items below the durability penalty threshold erodes trust (-2% per broken item sold, "Pawnbroker" debuff). At reputation milestones (50, 150, 300 transactions in a town), the merchant unlocks exclusive buy-back offers -- a rotating daily selection of 2-3 items previously sold by that player, purchasable at 150% of the original sell price. This creates a "seller's remorse rescue valve" for players who accidentally sold a good item, while the markup keeps it from being a free undo. The implementation layers cleanly onto `sellService.ts` where sell price is already computed via `calculateSellPrice` -- the reputation modifier applies as a final multiplier, and the sold item's metadata is written to a small ring buffer in Redis (last 20 items per town) for the buy-back catalog. The frontend surfaces reputation as a subtle merchant mood icon on the sell confirmation dialog ("The merchant grins eagerly" vs "The merchant eyes your wares suspiciously"), giving flavor feedback without requiring a new screen. This transforms selling from a mindless gold-conversion tap into a system with memory, personality, and consequences -- players start thinking about which town to sell in and what to sell there, creating emergent trade route behavior that complements the Worn Path trade routes idea at the macro level while operating at the micro level of individual transactions.
