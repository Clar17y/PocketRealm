# Battle-Worn Tempering: Durability-Based Item Growth

**Category:** feature
**Priority:** medium
**Scope:** medium

## Description
Equipment that survives repeated combats without breaking accumulates a hidden "temper" counter. Once an item crosses temper milestones (e.g., 50, 150, 300 combats survived), it earns a small permanent bonus stat (+1 attack, +2 defence, etc.) tied to its slot type, displayed as a "Battle-Worn" badge on the item tooltip. This gives players a reason to invest in repairs and keep gear maintained rather than treating durability as a pure tax -- well-loved equipment literally gets stronger over time, creating an emotional attachment to items and adding a long-term progression layer that complements the existing forge upgrade path without replacing it.
