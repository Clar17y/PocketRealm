# Boss Role Identity: Emergent Class Fantasy from Fight Data

**Category:** feature
**Priority:** medium
**Scope:** medium

## Description

Analyze each player's cumulative BossParticipant history to derive a persistent "Boss Role Identity" -- a title and passive bonus that reflects how they actually behave in boss fights, not what they claim to be. The system examines lifetime aggregates across all boss encounters: players whose `damageAbsorbed / totalDamage` ratio exceeds a threshold are classified as "Bulwark" (tank identity), those with high `totalHealing / roundsSurvived` as "Lifeline" (healer identity), those with high `crits / hits` ratio as "Executioner" (burst DPS), and those with consistently high `totalDamage` but low `damageAbsorbed` as "Shadow" (sustained DPS who avoids aggro). Each identity grants a small passive bonus that activates only in boss encounters: Bulwark gets +5% threat generation, Lifeline gets +10% healing done, Executioner gets +8% crit damage, Shadow gets +5% damage when not the current aggro target. Identity recalculates after every boss kill based on the trailing 10 encounters, so players can shift roles organically by changing their template and playstyle rather than being locked in. The key insight is that the BossParticipant table already stores everything needed -- `totalDamage`, `totalHealing`, `damageAbsorbed`, `threat`, `hits`, `crits`, `attacks`, `status` -- but this rich behavioral data is only ever used once for loot distribution and then forgotten. Mining it for identity creates a class-like fantasy in a classless game, where your role is earned through action rather than selected at character creation, and gives players a reason to care about how they perform in boss fights beyond just "did I get loot." The frontend can display the current role identity as a badge on the boss signup screen and in guild member lists, creating social legibility ("we need another Bulwark for this boss") without imposing hard role requirements. Implementation is a lightweight derivation function in game-engine that takes an array of BossParticipant records, computes ratio scores, and returns the dominant identity, cached in Redis and refreshed post-encounter.
