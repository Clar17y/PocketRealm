# Achievement Flavor: Exploration Milestones

## Content

### Discovery Track

#### Wanderer (3 zones discovered, +1000 turns)

*"Three zones. The Forest Edge, and then two more places that the Forest Edge did not prepare you for. The Pocketrealm is larger than it looked from Millbrook's gate, and every new horizon makes the last one feel smaller."*

Unlock text: You have seen enough to know there is more. The world just got wider, and it is not done yet.

#### Pathfinder (5 zones discovered, title: The Pathfinder)

*"Five zones mapped in your mind, five sets of dangers catalogued, five places where the locals have learned your footsteps. The paths between them are becoming familiar, which is the Pocketrealm's way of telling you it is time to find harder ones."*

Unlock text: Half the world is open to you. The other half is waiting to see if you are serious.

#### Cartographer (all 11 zones discovered, title: The Cartographer, +2 attribute points)

*"Every zone, from the sunlit farms beyond Millbrook to the drowned corridors of the Sunken Ruins. You have stood in places that most adventurers only hear about in Maren's tavern, and you walked there on your own feet. The Pocketrealm has no more secrets of geography to offer you. Only the secrets that live inside them."*

Unlock text: You have seen it all. Every forest, every cave, every ruin. The map is complete. What you do with it is your business.

### Full Exploration Track

#### Thorough (1 zone fully explored)

*"One zone, turned inside out. Every path walked, every corner checked, every tier uncovered. Most adventurers pass through. You stayed until there was nothing left to find, which takes a particular kind of stubbornness that the Pocketrealm respects."*

Unlock text: You do not leave loose ends. One zone has given up all its secrets to you.

#### Surveyor (3 zones fully explored, title: The Surveyor)

*"Three zones stripped bare. You know where the copper veins run thin, where the wolves change patrol routes, and which goblin tunnels loop back on themselves. You have moved past exploration and into something closer to ownership."*

Unlock text: Three zones, fully mapped, fully understood. The wilderness bends around your knowledge of it.

#### World Walker (all 9 wild zones fully explored, title: World Walker, +3 attribute points)

*"Nine wild zones. Every exploration tier reached, every hidden corner illuminated, every ambient threat accounted for. You have walked from the gentle oaks of the Forest Edge to the abyssal dark of the Sunken Ruins and left nothing undiscovered behind you. The Pocketrealm has been measured, and the one holding the ruler is you."*

Unlock text: There is not a single square foot of wilderness you have not claimed with your presence. The world is mapped. You are the World Walker.

## Integration Notes

- **Same display pattern as A-01, A-02, A-03:** Short unlock text as toast/popup; italic passage in achievement detail view.
- **Two distinct tracks:** Discovery achievements reward breadth (finding new zones), while full exploration achievements reward depth (completing all tiers within a zone). The writing reflects this: discovery flavor is about horizons and scale, exploration flavor is about thoroughness and mastery.
- **Zone count context:** There are 11 total zones (2 towns + 9 wild). Cartographer requires all 11. World Walker requires fully exploring all 9 wild zones (towns have no exploration tiers).
- **Cross-references:** Cartographer mentions Maren's tavern (N-03) as where adventurers hear stories. Surveyor references copper veins (Forest Edge Z-02), wolf patrols (wolves M-04), and goblin tunnels (Cave Entrance Z-04). World Walker bookends the journey from Forest Edge to Sunken Ruins.
- **Reward context:** Wanderer's 1000 turn reward is referenced obliquely ("the world just got wider"). The attribute point rewards for Cartographer and World Walker are not mentioned in flavor (mechanical rewards stay mechanical), but the tier 3 writing carries the weight of the accomplishment.
- **Tone:** Exploration achievements are more contemplative than combat ones. The humour is gentler, leaning on the satisfaction of completionism rather than escalating absurdity.
