# Item Descriptions: Soulbound Gear

## Content

**Rat Hide Gloves**
Your first real armour, stitched from rat pelts by someone (probably you) who had never worked leather before, and it shows. They fit well enough, protect your knuckles from things that bite, and smell faintly of the Forest Edge no matter how many times you wash them.

**Spider Silk Belt**
A strip of woven spider silk cinched at the waist, light enough to forget you are wearing it and strong enough to remind you when something hits you there. The silk's faint stickiness has not gone away, and Vesper maintains that this is a feature.

**Boar Hide Boots**
Sturdy boots cut from Forest Edge boar hide, thick-soled and practical and utterly lacking in elegance. They will keep your feet dry, absorb the kind of impacts Kessa would consider negligible, and outlast your first dozen fights without complaint.

**Wayfinder Buckler**
A small, round shield issued by the Millbrook militia to every adventurer who walks through the gate, stamped with a compass rose that Fen Darrow insists is decorative. The angled grip steadies your off-hand and improves your aim, which is why it has saved more lives than any weapon in Millbrook's armoury.

## Integration Notes

- Add as `flavorText` on the `ItemTemplate` model, keyed by the `IDS.adv.*` and `STARTER_LOADOUT.tutorialOffHandTemplateId` item IDs.
- **Soulbound, unsellable starter gear.** All four items have sellPrice 0 and tier 1. The descriptions reflect this: humble, practical, self-deprecating. These are the items you wear before you know what you are doing, and the tone treats them with affectionate honesty rather than the reverence of later tiers.
- **Material callbacks:** Each item connects to a T1 mob drop (I-04): Rat Hide Gloves from Rat Pelt, Spider Silk Belt from Spider Silk, Boar Hide Boots from Boar Hide. The descriptions assume the player has encountered (and killed) the source creatures, grounding the gear in the early Forest Edge experience.
- **Cross-references:**
  - Rat Hide Gloves: "someone (probably you)" acknowledges the player's first crafting attempt and connects to the self-deprecating tone of the Rat Pelt description (I-04). The lingering Forest Edge smell callbacks the zone's atmosphere (Z-02).
  - Spider Silk Belt references Vesper Tain (N-04) and her clinical insistence that the stickiness is a feature, consistent with her pattern of reframing unsettling properties as benefits (see also: Venomweave Boots in I-16, spider silk "remembering").
  - Boar Hide Boots references Kessa Ironweld (N-02) through her standards (calling the impacts "negligible"), echoing her blunt assessment style without direct dialogue.
  - Wayfinder Buckler references Captain Fen Darrow (N-11) by name and the Millbrook militia. The compass rose detail connects to the gate (Z-01) and Fen Darrow's protectiveness. The accuracy stat (12, unusually high for T1) is narratively explained by the angled grip, making the tutorial item's mechanical purpose feel intentional rather than arbitrary.
- **Wayfinder Buckler as tutorial item:** This is the `STARTER_LOADOUT.tutorialOffHandTemplateId`, meaning every player starts with it. The description treats it as institutional: Millbrook issues it, Fen Darrow endorses it, and its purpose is keeping new adventurers alive long enough to find something better. The line "saved more lives than any weapon" frames defence as the first lesson of the Pocketrealm.
- **Tone tier:** These are T1 descriptions in the T1 emotional register: self-aware, modest, and slightly embarrassed. They are the opposite of T5's reverence. The player should look at these items and think "I started here," with a mix of nostalgia and relief.
