# Achievement Flavor: Gathering Milestones

## Content

### Prospector (10 gathering actions)

*"Ten swings, ten harvests, ten small conversations with the ground. You have copper dust under your nails, sap on your sleeves, and herb stains on your fingers. Rowan Delk would say this is what 'starting' looks like. He would be right."*

Unlock text: Ten gathers. Your hands are learning what your eyes cannot teach them. This is how it begins.

### Harvester (100 gathering actions)

*"A hundred times you have knelt in the dirt, struck stone, or stripped bark, and a hundred times the Pocketrealm has given you something in return. The work is repetitive. The work is unglamorous. The work is also the reason Millbrook has ingots, planks, and potions, which makes it more important than any single fight you have ever won."*

Unlock text: One hundred gathers. The land knows the sound of your tools. You are no longer a visitor; you are a regular.

### Resource Baron (500 gathering actions, title: The Harvester)

*"Five hundred. The copper hollows know the shape of your pick. The oaks lean away when you approach (though that might be the treants). The herb patches recognize your step and have, Vesper suspects, begun growing slightly faster in your favourite spots, which is either a compliment or a bribe."*

Unlock text: Five hundred times you asked the land for something, and five hundred times it answered. The title is earned through patience, not glory. That makes it rarer.

### Strip Miner (1,000 gathering actions, title: Strip Miner, +1 attribute point)

*"One thousand. The number is staggering when you say it aloud, and entirely reasonable when you consider how many ingots, planks, and potions the Pocketrealm consumes in a single week. You are the supply chain. You are the reason Kessa's forge stays lit, Vesper's bench stays stocked, and Bram's shelves stay full. Without combat, the Pocketrealm would be dangerous. Without gathering, it would be empty."*

Unlock text: A thousand gathers. The Pocketrealm's economy rests on your shoulders, your calluses, and your willingness to hit one more rock. Strip Miner is not a title. It is a job description, and you have earned it.

## Integration Notes

- **Same display pattern as all previous achievement entries:** Short unlock text as toast/popup; italic passage in achievement detail view.
- **Single escalating chain:** Unlike combat (kill count + boss + PvP) or exploration (discovery + full explore), gathering has one linear track. The writing reflects this by building a single narrative arc: from tentative beginnings (Prospector) through quiet competence (Harvester) to economic indispensability (Strip Miner).
- **Cross-references:**
  - Prospector references Rowan Delk (N-06) as the standard-bearer for gathering.
  - Resource Baron references the Copper Hollows (Z-02), treants (M-06, the oaks leaning away joke), and Vesper Tain (N-04, noticing herb growth patterns).
  - Strip Miner references Kessa (N-02, forge), Vesper (N-04, bench), and Bram (N-01, shelves), positioning the gatherer as the economic foundation that supports all three.
- **Tone:** Gathering achievements are the quietest in the game. No kills, no bosses, no PvP. The writing leans into the dignity of repetitive work, finding humour and meaning in the unglamorous act of hitting rocks and picking flowers. The final entry (Strip Miner) makes the case that gathering is the most important activity in the Pocketrealm, not through combat metaphors but through economic ones.
- **Progression feel:** Prospector is humble. Harvester is competent. Resource Baron is proud. Strip Miner is foundational. The arc goes from "I tried this" to "this is who I am."
