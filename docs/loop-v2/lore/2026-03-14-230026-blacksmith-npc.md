# NPC Dialogue: Blacksmith / Weapon Vendor (Millbrook)

## Content

### Character Brief

**Name:** Kessa Ironweld
**Location:** The Forge, Crafting Quarter (behind The Crooked Antler); weapons rack on the right side of Main Street
**Personality:** Direct, proud, and perpetually overheated. Kessa has the conversational patience of a short fuse and the professional standards of someone who knows that a poorly balanced blade gets people killed. She respects results, despises excuses, and speaks to everyone the same way: quickly, loudly, and while doing something else with her hands.

---

### Greeting Lines (on shop open)

> "You want something sharp, something sturdy, or something you can wave around to feel brave? I can do all three. Prices are on the rack. Don't touch anything until you've decided."

> "Back again? Good. Means the last thing I sold you worked. Or it didn't and you're a ghost. Either way, let's see your coin."

> "Make it quick. I've got ingots on the anvil and they don't care about your schedule."

### Browse / Idle Lines (while shop is open)

> "That copper dagger? Fine work, if I say so. And I do say so, because I made it. Holds an edge for about a week, which is longer than most adventurers last."

> "If you're eyeing the wooden swords, those are from the carpenter, not me. I just sell them because he can't be bothered. And yes, before you ask, they're terrible. But they're cheap and they're here."

> "See those burn marks on the wall? That's what happens when an apprentice tries to quench dark iron in cold water instead of oil. The apprentice is fine. The wall is not."

### Purchase Lines (on buy)

> "Good steel finds good hands. Or at least it finds hands. Yours will do."

> "Sold. If you bring it back bent, I'll straighten it. If you bring it back broken, I'll ask questions you won't like."

> "There you go. Swing it before you trust it. Every blade has a personality, and some of them are disagreeable."

### Sell / Salvage Lines (player selling or salvaging)

> "Hmm. Decent material under all that damage. I'll melt it down and make something better. That's the circle of iron."

> "You want me to buy this? I forged this. I know exactly what it's worth, which is why my price is fair and your disappointment is your own problem."

> "Salvage? Absolutely. I'll strip it to components. Less sentimental than selling, more useful than hoarding. Smart choice."

### Forge Upgrade Lines (on forge upgrade interactions)

> "Upgrading, are we? Good. Nothing sadder than a weapon that's peaked early. Hold still, don't breathe on the metal, and let me work."

> "Every upgrade is a gamble. I do the skill part; the universe handles the luck part. We split the blame accordingly."

### Farewell Lines (on shop close)

> "Go on, then. Hit something with it. That's what it's for."

> "Stay sharp out there. And I mean that literally. Bring a whetstone."

## Integration Notes

- **Same dialogue system as N-01 (Bram Holloway):** Keyed by NPC ID and event type (greeting, idle, buy, sell, farewell), with random selection. Add "forge_upgrade" as an additional event type for Kessa.
- **Location:** Her forge is in the Crafting Quarter (Z-01), but her weapons rack is on Main Street's right side (Z-01). She could appear in either location depending on UI context.
- **Cross-references:** The copper dagger idle line connects to the I-01 weapons lore. The wooden sword line explains why a blacksmith sells a carpenter's product. The dark iron burn mark story foreshadows T4 materials. The salvage lines connect to the crafting/salvage system.
- **Personality contrast with Bram:** Where Bram is slow and amused, Kessa is fast and impatient. They should feel like neighboring shopkeepers who tolerate each other, which could fuel incidental dialogue between them in future NPC entries.
- **Forge upgrade lines:** Display when the player uses the forge upgrade feature (from the `CRAFTING_CONSTANTS` system). These are unique to Kessa's shop and don't apply to general merchants.
- **Name (Kessa Ironweld):** Reusable in quest text, other NPC references, and the forge UI header.
