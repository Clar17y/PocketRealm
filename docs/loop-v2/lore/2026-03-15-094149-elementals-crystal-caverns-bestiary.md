# Mob Flavor Text: Elementals (Crystal Caverns)

## Content

### Family Overview

The elementals of the Crystal Caverns are not creatures. They are phenomena. Where golems are shaped by the caverns' minerals and goblins have simply moved in, the elementals are the arcane energy of the Crystal Caverns made manifest: condensed geometry, crystallised magic, and the caverns' ambient hum given form, purpose, and the inclination to destroy anything that disrupts the resonance. They are not alive in any sense that Vesper Tain can classify (and she has tried, extensively, from a safe distance). They do not eat, sleep, reproduce, or decay. They simply are, and they have been since the crystals first began to glow.

Vesper's working theory is that the elementals are the caverns' immune system: a response to disturbance, calibrated to the frequency of whatever disturbed them. This would explain why they ignore the golems (same frequency), tolerate the goblins (low threat), and attack adventurers on sight (foreign resonance). It does not explain the Crystal Titan, which appears to generate resonance rather than respond to it, and which Vesper describes as "less an immune response and more a cathedral that decided to stand up."

---

### Shard Elemental
*Appearance:* A floating cluster of crystal fragments arranged in a vaguely humanoid silhouette, held together by visible lines of arcane force that crackle faintly between the shards. The fragments rotate slowly around a central core of pale light, and the light intensifies when it detects your presence. It has no face, no limbs, and no discernible anatomy. It has geometry, and the geometry is hostile.
*Behavior:* Shard elementals drift through the caverns' mid-levels, drawn to sources of arcane disturbance (which, in practice, means anything that is not a crystal). Crystal Shard on round 3 fires a fragment from the cluster at high velocity, dealing 6 magic damage. Between spells, it deals magic damage through proximity: the shards vibrate at frequencies that disrupt biological matter in ways that Vesper describes as "fascinating" and adventurers describe as "painful."
*Lore:* Shard elementals form spontaneously in areas of high crystal density, condensing from ambient arcane energy the way dew condenses from humid air. They dissipate when defeated, their fragments scattering across the floor and losing their glow within minutes. The fragments are inert afterwards. Vesper has a collection of them. She says they are "just crystals now," in a tone that suggests she is disappointed.
*Combat Feel:* Magic-damage glass cannon. Accuracy 18 and magic defence 18 are high for tier 2, but HP 40 and physical defence 14 are modest. The shard elemental hits harder than it looks (magic damage bypasses physical armour) and dies faster than it should (physical attacks connect easily). Crystal Shard on round 3 adds a damage spike. Kill it quickly; it has no healing and no sustain.

### Crystal Wisp
*Appearance:* A small, floating orb of cold, blue-white light, approximately the size of a fist, trailing luminous threads that dissolve into the air behind it. It moves through the caverns with the drifting, unhurried motion of something that has nowhere to be and infinite time to not be there. It looks harmless. It looks beautiful. Neither of these observations is accurate.
*Behavior:* Crystal wisps are the simplest elementals: no spells, no debuffs, just magic damage delivered through contact with the luminous threads they trail. They drift through passages in loose groups, their light making them visible from a distance, which is either a warning or an invitation depending on your perspective. In combat, they are fragile (HP 30, defence 10) and evasive (12, the highest in the elemental family), flickering in and out of visibility as they reposition between attacks.
*Lore:* Crystal wisps are the caverns' most common elemental and the reason that first-time visitors think the Crystal Caverns are safe. They look like decoration. The light they cast is genuinely pleasant. Rowan Delk, who has gathered Shimmer Fern in the upper grottos, says the wisps "improve the ambiance considerably, right up to the moment they stop being ambient and start being personal."
*Combat Feel:* High evasion (12), low everything else. The wisp is the elemental family's dodge tank: difficult to hit, trivial to kill once you connect. Magic damage is moderate (4-8) and sustained through auto-attacks rather than spells. The frustration is missing repeatedly against something that looks like a nightlight. Accuracy is the only stat that matters.

### Storm Crystal
*Appearance:* A jagged, freestanding crystal formation that has pulled itself from the cavern wall and begun to move. It is roughly humanoid in shape, but the proportions are wrong: too angular, too faceted, and crackling with arcs of electrical energy that jump between its surfaces in patterns that look random but repeat if you watch long enough. The air around a storm crystal smells of ozone and tastes of metal, and the hum it produces is loud enough to feel in your teeth.
*Behavior:* Storm crystals patrol the deep chambers where the caverns' arcane density is highest, moving with a grinding deliberation that belies the speed of their attacks. Lightning Arc on round 3 fires a bolt of crystallised electrical energy that deals 9 magic damage and illuminates the entire chamber in a flash that leaves afterimages. Static Field on round 5 creates a zone of disrupted energy that drops your evasion by 4 for two rounds, pinning you in place for whatever follows. Between spells, the storm crystal hits with magic damage and an accuracy (20) that suggests it knows where you are going before you do.
*Lore:* Vesper believes storm crystals are shard elementals that reached a critical mass of absorbed energy and crystallised into a more stable form. If she is right, the Crystal Caverns are a lifecycle: ambient energy becomes wisps, wisps become shards, shards become storms. She has not observed the transition directly. She would very much like to, from behind something thick.
*Combat Feel:* The elite. HP 55, defence 18, magic defence 22, accuracy 20, and a two-spell rotation that combines damage with evasion reduction. Lightning Arc (round 3) hits hard. Static Field (round 5) strips your ability to dodge, which matters because the storm crystal's auto-attack accuracy is already high. The combination creates a mid-fight window where everything connects and nothing misses. Physical damage is the counter (defence 18 is moderate), but the magic damage output means you take significant punishment regardless.

### Crystal Titan (Boss)
*Appearance:* The Crystal Titan is the Resonance Chamber made ambulant. A towering figure of interlocking crystal plates, each one singing at a different frequency, assembled into a form that is roughly humanoid in the way that a cathedral is roughly a building. Its core is a sphere of concentrated light so bright that looking directly at it leaves a coloured afterimage that does not fade for minutes. The cavern hums when it moves, and the hum is not the caverns responding to the Titan. The Titan is the source. It is the hum, given shape, and the shape has decided that you do not belong here.
*Behavior:* The Crystal Titan fights with a four-spell escalation that is the longest and most damaging magic rotation in the Crystal Caverns. Resonance on round 2 buffs its own attack by 5 for three rounds, amplifying everything that follows. Crystal Storm on round 4 fires a barrage of crystal fragments for 12 magic damage during the buff window. Prism Beam on round 6 delivers 16 magic damage in a focused beam of refracted light that Vesper has described as "the most beautiful thing I never want to see pointed at me." Shatter All on round 8 deals 22 magic damage, the highest single-hit magic damage in the tier 4 zones: a detonation of focused resonance that turns the air into a wall of crystallised force.
*Lore:* The Crystal Titan does not patrol. It resides. It occupies the Resonance Chamber at the caverns' heart, and its presence amplifies the ambient hum to a visible shimmer that can be felt from chambers away. It is the only elemental that other elementals orbit, drawn to its resonance the way smaller crystals align to a larger formation. The Mysterious Stranger, passing through the caverns on one of their rarer appearances, said of the Titan: "It is not alive. It is not dead. It is the third thing, the one that the caverns are still deciding." Kessa Ironweld, who has seen the mithril veins pulse in time with the Titan's movements, says simply: "Do not mine near it. The stone objects."
*Combat Feel:* The elemental boss. HP 90, defence 24, magic defence 28 (the highest in the zone), accuracy 22, and a four-spell rotation that escalates through buff, barrage, beam, and detonation. Resonance (round 2) amplifies subsequent damage. Crystal Storm (round 4) hits during the buff. Prism Beam (round 6) hits harder. Shatter All (round 8) hits hardest. The Titan is the Pocketrealm's purest magic-damage boss: every attack is magical, every defence is arcane, and physical armour is essentially decorative. Magic defence is the survival stat. Physical damage is the kill stat (defence 24 is high but not golem-tier). The fight is a DPS race against an escalating timer, because every spell hits harder than the last, and Shatter All at 22 damage is the point of no return.
