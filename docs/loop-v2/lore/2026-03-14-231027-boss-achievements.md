# Achievement Flavor: Boss Kill Milestones

## Content

### Boss Hunter (1 boss kill)

*"Your first boss. The ground shook, the air tasted like copper, and for one clarifying moment you understood exactly how small you are. Then you won anyway. That is the part that matters."*

Unlock text: You looked something terrible in the eye and it blinked first. Welcome to the deep end.

### Boss Crusher (10 boss kills, title: Bane of Bosses, +1 attribute point)

*"Ten bosses felled. You have developed a routine for this: scout, prepare, engage, survive. The preparation takes longer than the killing now, which says more about you than any title could. The creatures that rule the wild places have started to recognize you, and recognition is the first step toward fear."*

Unlock text: Ten of the Pocketrealm's worst, brought low. When lesser creatures see you coming, they have started stepping aside. Wise of them.

### Raid Leader (25 boss kills, title: Raid Leader, +2 attribute points)

*"Twenty-five. You have fought the Alpha Wolf in its den and the Ancient Spirit in its sanctum. You have stood in places where the world bends under the weight of old, furious things, and you walked out carrying their trophies. The Pocketrealm does not hand out legends. You carved this one yourself, encounter by encounter, each one a story that nobody who wasn't there would believe."*

Unlock text: Others follow where you lead, because you have already been everywhere worth going and killed everything worth killing. The title is a formality. The reputation came first.

## Integration Notes

- **Same display pattern as A-01:** Short unlock text as a toast/popup on achievement trigger; italic expanded passage in the achievement detail/inspection view.
- **Tone contrast with A-01:** Kill milestones (A-01) escalate through absurdity (ending at "natural disaster"). Boss milestones stay grounded and personal, because each boss is a distinct, memorable fight rather than an anonymous counter.
- **Cross-references:** "Alpha Wolf in its den" and "Ancient Spirit in its sanctum" name two confirmed bosses from the mob data. Future bestiary entries will add more, but these two anchor the flavor in known encounters.
- **Title context:** "Bane of Bosses" and "Raid Leader" are cosmetic titles displayed on the player's profile. The flavor text gives narrative weight to why these titles feel earned.
- **Scaling:** The three tiers (1, 10, 25) are much tighter than the kill chain (100 to 10,000), reflecting that boss kills are rare events. The writing reflects this: each entry carries more gravity per threshold point.
