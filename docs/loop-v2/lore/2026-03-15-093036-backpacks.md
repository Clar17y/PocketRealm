# Item Descriptions: Backpacks

## Content

**Cloth Satchel**
A simple bag of stitched canvas with a drawstring top, sold by Bram for a price that suggests he knows you have no alternative. Holds eight items, which sounds like plenty until it is not.

**Reinforced Pack**
Canvas reinforced with boar leather straps and copper buckles, sturdy enough to survive being dragged through the Forest Edge and back. Sixteen slots, which is the point where carrying capacity stops being a limitation and starts being a decision.

**Traveller's Rucksack**
A properly framed pack with internal compartments, waxed canvas, and adjustable straps that Rowan Delk helped design (he insisted the hip belt distribute weight evenly, because he has opinions about spinal health). Twenty-four slots for the adventurer who has committed to being away from town for longer than a day.

**Ranger's Haversack**
Built from willow-frame construction and lined with spider silk for waterproofing, designed for Thornwall's long-range patrols and adopted by anyone who spends more time in the field than in town. Thirty-two slots, organized for rapid access without removing the pack, because Lira Caravel does not believe in packing inefficiently and neither should you.

**Adventurer's Expedition Pack**
The largest portable storage solution in the Pocketrealm: forty slots of waxed canvas, mithril-reinforced framing, and compartmentalized organization that Aldric Voss would approve of if he ever left his desk long enough to see one in the field. It weighs more than some weapons, but it carries everything, which is the point.

## Integration Notes

- Add as `flavorText` on the `ItemTemplate` model, keyed by the `IDS.backpack.*` item IDs.
- **Backpacks are pure utility.** No durability, no level requirement, no combat stats. The descriptions reflect this: they are about carrying things, and the tone is practical rather than martial. The humor comes from the mundane reality that inventory management is the Pocketrealm's truest constant.
- **Slot counts in descriptions:** Each description mentions its slot count explicitly, because the number is the item's entire identity. The phrasing escalates from limitation ("sounds like plenty until it is not") through sufficiency ("stops being a limitation") to abundance ("carries everything").
- **Cross-references:**
  - Bram Holloway (N-01) anchors the Cloth Satchel, selling the starter bag with the same pragmatic monopoly he brings to everything in the general store.
  - Rowan Delk (N-06) anchors the Traveller's Rucksack with ergonomic opinions, consistent with his practical survivalist character. His concern for spinal health in a world of monsters is the entry's comedic beat.
  - Lira Caravel (N-09) anchors the Ranger's Haversack through Thornwall's patrol supply chain, connecting the backpack to the frontier town's military logistics. Her efficiency standards extend to how things are packed, not just what is packed.
  - Aldric Voss (N-05) anchors the Expedition Pack through his organizational instincts, with the observation that he would approve of the compartmentalization if he ever saw one outside of a filing cabinet.
  - Material progression mirrors the world: canvas (T1), boar leather and copper (T2), waxed canvas with proper framing (T3), willow-frame and spider silk waterproofing (T4), mithril-reinforced (T5). Each tier uses materials from its corresponding zone tier.
- **No Kessa or Vesper:** Backpacks are not weapons or potions, so the two artisan NPCs are deliberately absent. The backpack NPC references skew toward the logistics and exploration characters (Bram, Rowan, Lira, Aldric), reflecting who would actually care about carrying capacity.
