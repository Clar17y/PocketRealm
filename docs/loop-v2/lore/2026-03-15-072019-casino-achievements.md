# Achievement Flavor: Casino Milestones

## Content

### Wagering Track

#### Feeling Lucky (first bet placed)

*"Your first bet. A small number pushed across the table toward a man who smiles for a living. Silas Vane welcomed you to The Gilded Wheel with the warmth of someone who has just acquired a new source of income. Whether you won or lost is beside the point. You sat down. That is the only decision that matters, and it is the one you cannot take back."*

Unlock text: Your first wager. The wheel spins for you now. It will spin regardless, but now it does so with your money on the line.

#### High Roller (100,000 gold wagered total, tier 2)

*"One hundred thousand gold, fed to the wheel one bet at a time. You have developed a rhythm: the exchange, the wager, the spin, the result. Win or lose, the process is the same, and you have found a comfort in the repetition that says more about you than the outcome ever could. Silas has started greeting you by name. That is either hospitality or a warning sign."*

Unlock text: A hundred thousand wagered. The wheel knows your face, your habits, and the exact weight of your favourite bet. Welcome to the deep end.

#### Whale (1,000,000 gold wagered, title: Whale, tier 3)

*"One million gold through the wheel. The number is staggering, and Silas handles it with the casual grace of a man who has seen staggering numbers before and found them all equally useful. You are a whale: the casino's most valued species, distinguished by volume rather than luck. The title sounds like mockery. It is not. Whales keep the lights on, and The Gilded Wheel's lights have never been brighter."*

Unlock text: A million gold wagered. Whale. You are not a gambler anymore. You are an institution, and institutions have titles.

#### The House (10,000,000 gold wagered, title: The House, tier 5, +3 attribute points)

*"Ten million gold. The number has transcended money and become a philosophical statement about the nature of entertainment, risk, and the peculiar human impulse to convert certainty into possibility and call the exchange worthwhile. Silas Vane does not smile when you sit down anymore. He nods. The nod says: you and I are the same. We both live by the wheel. The difference is that I built it and you keep spinning it, and neither of us can stop. The House always wins. But which house are we talking about?"*

Unlock text: Ten million. The House. The title belongs to the casino, and now it belongs to you. Silas considers this a partnership. He has never been more sincere.

### Wealth Track

#### Jackpot (100,000 gold held at once, tier 2)

*"One hundred thousand gold in your possession at the same time. Not wagered, not spent, not lost. Held. The weight of it (metaphorical, since gold in the Pocketrealm does not actually weigh anything, which is a separate conversation) changes how you walk through Thornwall. Lira Caravel treats you differently. Silas watches you more carefully. Having money is a statement. Having this much money is an argument."*

Unlock text: A hundred thousand gold, all at once. Jackpot. For one shining moment, you are the richest person in the room. Enjoy it before the wheel gets ideas.

#### Fortune (1,000,000 gold held at once, title: Fortunate, tier 4, +3 attribute points)

*"One million gold. At once. In your pocket (again, metaphorically; the pocket in question would need to be geological). You have achieved a level of wealth that Bram Holloway cannot comprehend, Lira Caravel envies professionally, and Silas Vane considers a personal challenge. The gold is not the achievement. The restraint required to accumulate it without spending it is. In a world that asks you to invest every resource into survival, you found a way to hoard a million of them and still be standing. That is not luck. That is discipline wearing a very expensive hat."*

Unlock text: A million gold, held at the same time. Fortune. The title is earned not by spending but by not spending, which in the Pocketrealm is the harder skill by far.

## Integration Notes

- **Same display pattern as all previous achievement entries:** Short unlock text as toast/popup; italic passage in achievement detail view.
- **Two tracks:** Wagering achievements reward the act of gambling (putting gold through the wheel), while wealth achievements reward accumulation (holding gold). The tones are deliberately different: wagering entries have the seductive pull of the casino, while wealth entries have the sober satisfaction of restraint.
- **Cross-references:**
  - Silas Vane (N-07) anchors the wagering track, with his reactions escalating from warm welcome (Feeling Lucky) through casual management (High Roller) to existential partnership (The House).
  - The Gilded Wheel (Z-08) is referenced as the casino's named establishment.
  - Lira Caravel (N-09) and Bram Holloway (N-01) are referenced in the wealth track, grounding gold totals in the NPC economy.
- **The House (tier 5):** One of only two tier 5 achievements (alongside Golden Hands). Where Golden Hands is about a single miraculous moment, The House is about sustained, accumulated excess. Both are endgame achievements, but they celebrate opposite philosophies: creation vs. consumption.
- **Tone:** Casino achievements are the only category where the humour shades into something darker. The writing acknowledges that gambling is seductive, repetitive, and self-reinforcing, without moralising about it. Silas's evolving reactions (warmth > familiarity > kinship) mirror the player's deepening relationship with the wheel. The final line of The House ("which house are we talking about?") is the lore's most self-aware moment.
- **Achievements complete:** This is the 12th and final achievement category. All 12 achievement entries are done.
