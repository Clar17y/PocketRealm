# Achievement Flavor: Skills and Level Milestones

## Content

### Skill Level Track

#### Dedicated (level 10 in any skill)

*"Ten levels in a single discipline. What began as fumbling has become competence, and competence, in the Pocketrealm, is the difference between coming home and not coming home. Rowan Delk would nod at this. Kessa Ironweld might too, though she would pretend she didn't."*

Unlock text: Level 10. You have stopped being a beginner and started being dangerous. The skill knows your name now.

#### Specialist (level 25 in any skill, title: The Specialist)

*"Twenty-five levels of deliberate, focused practice. You have chosen your path and walked it past the point where most people turn back, through the plateau where progress feels impossible and out the other side into territory that belongs to the committed. Your hands do things your mind has not yet named. Your instincts have outpaced your understanding, which is exactly how mastery begins."*

Unlock text: Level 25. You are no longer learning a skill. You are becoming it.

#### Master (level 50 in any skill, title: The Master, +2 attribute points)

*"Fifty. The number does not do it justice. Fifty levels is ten thousand repetitions, a hundred failed experiments, and one long, quiet conversation with a discipline that has decided to let you in on its secrets. Vesper Tain reached it with alchemy and said the world looked different afterwards, as if she had been reading everything through a dirty window until the day it came clean. Whether you reached fifty with a blade, a pickaxe, or a spell, the feeling is the same: you are no longer practicing. You are fluent."*

Unlock text: Level 50. Mastery. The word used to feel aspirational. Now it fits like a glove you have worn thin from use.

### Character Level Track

#### Rising Star (character level 10)

*"Level 10. The Pocketrealm's way of confirming that you are, in fact, not going away. Your skills have broadened, your stats have grown, and the creatures near Millbrook have revised their opinions about you. The forest rats no longer bother. The wolves are still deciding."*

Unlock text: Character level 10. You have outgrown the starting gear and started earning the attention of things that do not eat rats.

#### Veteran (character level 25, title: Veteran, +1 attribute point)

*"Twenty-five levels of accumulated experience, worn like armour and visible in the way you move. New adventurers ask you for advice at The Crooked Antler. Maren serves your drink before you sit down. Aldric Voss files your quest completions without checking the details, because your numbers always add up. You have become a fixture, and fixtures in the Pocketrealm tend to last."*

Unlock text: Level 25. The Pocketrealm has stopped testing you and started relying on you. That is either an honour or a warning.

#### Legend (character level 50, title: The Legend, +3 attribute points)

*"Fifty. You have stood in every zone, faced every family of creature, and survived the kind of encounters that Maren tells stories about to adventurers who have not yet left Millbrook. The number is a milestone, but the title is the truth: you are a legend, not because someone declared it, but because the Pocketrealm ran out of things to throw at you and you are still standing. Bram adds your name to the good customer list. Kessa nods when you walk past the forge. Rowan Delk, for the first time anyone can remember, looks impressed."*

Unlock text: Level 50. Legend. The title is not given. It is what remains after everything else has been tested, broken, rebuilt, and proven.

## Integration Notes

- **Same display pattern as all previous achievement entries:** Short unlock text as toast/popup; italic passage in achievement detail view.
- **Two tracks, two perspectives:** Skill level achievements focus inward (mastery of a single discipline, the feeling of competence deepening). Character level achievements focus outward (how the world and its NPCs respond to your growing presence).
- **Cross-references:**
  - Dedicated references Rowan Delk (N-06) and Kessa Ironweld (N-02) as arbiters of effort.
  - Master references Vesper Tain (N-04) as someone who has achieved level 50 in alchemy, grounding the achievement in an NPC the player knows.
  - Rising Star references the progression from rats (Forest Edge) to wolves (Deep Forest).
  - Veteran references Maren (N-03), Aldric (N-05), and The Crooked Antler (Z-01).
  - Legend references all six core Millbrook NPCs (Bram, Kessa, Maren, Vesper, Aldric, Rowan) reacting in character, creating a full-circle moment that rewards the player's investment in the world.
- **NPC ensemble moment:** Like the Legendary Smith crafting achievement (A-05), the Legend achievement uses multiple NPC reactions to make the milestone feel communal. Here it is expanded to all six NPCs, because character level 50 is the game's broadest accomplishment.
- **Skill types:** The skill track deliberately avoids naming specific skills (melee, mining, etc.) so the flavor applies regardless of which skill the player maxed first.
