# Achievement Flavor: Bestiary Milestones

## Content

### Unique Encounter Track

#### Naturalist (10 unique monsters encountered)

*"Ten species catalogued: their habits observed, their weaknesses noted, their tendency to bite recorded in careful detail. You have begun to see the Pocketrealm not as a series of threats but as an ecosystem, one where everything has a role and most of those roles involve trying to eat you."*

Unlock text: Ten unique creatures studied. The bestiary is filling in, and the patterns are starting to make a terrible kind of sense.

#### Monster Scholar (25 unique monsters encountered, title: The Scholar)

*"Twenty-five species. You can identify a Dire Wolf by its gait, a Goblin Warrior by its armour's rattle, and a Venomous Spider by the split second of silence before the bite. Vesper Tain has started citing your field notes in her research. Aldric Voss has started filing them. You are becoming, against all probability, an academic."*

Unlock text: Twenty-five. You know the Pocketrealm's creatures by name, by habit, and by the specific sound they make when they realize you are not afraid of them anymore.

### Mastery Track

#### Completionist (1 mob fully mastered)

*"One creature, studied completely. Every variant encountered, every prefix catalogued, every behaviour pattern documented. You have looked at this thing from every possible angle, and you now understand it better than it understands itself. There is a word for that kind of knowledge. The word is 'dangerous.'"*

Unlock text: One mob, fully mastered. You know it in your bones. Every prefix, every variant, every trick. It cannot surprise you anymore.

#### Zoologist (5 mobs fully mastered, title: The Zoologist, +1 attribute point)

*"Five species mastered in their entirety. Your bestiary entries read like autopsy reports written by someone who genuinely enjoys their work. Rowan Delk says you look at creatures the way he looks at ore veins: with assessment, not fear. He means it as a compliment. It might also be a concern."*

Unlock text: Five creatures, inside and out. The bestiary is becoming less of a field guide and more of a personal history, written in encounters and paid for in bruises.

#### Encyclopedist (all 50 mobs fully mastered, title: The Encyclopedist, +3 attribute points)

*"Every creature in the Pocketrealm, mastered. Every prefix encountered, every variant catalogued, every fight survived and studied. The bestiary is complete, and it is the most thorough document of its kind in existence. Scholars will reference it. Adventurers will carry copies. The creatures themselves, if they could read, would find it uncomfortably accurate. You did not just fight the Pocketrealm's bestiary. You became its author."*

Unlock text: All fifty, mastered. The Encyclopedist. Every creature in the Pocketrealm has been met, measured, and understood. The book is closed. You wrote it.

## Integration Notes

- **Same display pattern as all previous achievement entries:** Short unlock text as toast/popup; italic passage in achievement detail view.
- **Two tracks, two perspectives:** Unique encounters reward breadth (discovering new species). Mastery rewards depth (completing all prefixes for individual mobs). The writing mirrors this: encounter achievements are about recognition, mastery achievements are about exhaustive knowledge.
- **Cross-references:**
  - Monster Scholar references Vesper Tain (N-04, citing field notes) and Aldric Voss (N-05, filing them), grounding the scholarly achievement in the NPC network.
  - Monster Scholar names specific creatures (Dire Wolf from M-04, Goblin Warrior from M-08, Venomous Spider from M-02) to show that the knowledge is specific, not abstract.
  - Zoologist references Rowan Delk (N-06) comparing the player's assessment gaze to his own, connecting combat knowledge to gathering intuition.
- **Prefix system context:** "Mastering all prefixes" means encountering every variant (common, uncommon, rare, etc.) of a given mob. The flavor treats this as scholarly completionism rather than grind, which makes the achievement feel intentional rather than accidental.
- **Encyclopedist scale:** 50 mobs is the total mob template count. The writing acknowledges the scale without dwelling on the number, focusing instead on the result: the most complete bestiary in the Pocketrealm.
- **Tone:** Bestiary achievements occupy a unique space between combat (violence) and exploration (geography). They are about knowledge acquired through violence, which gives them a scholarly quality that is not quite clinical and not quite heroic. The humour comes from treating monster-fighting as field research.
