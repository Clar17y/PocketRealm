# Mob Flavor Text: Fae (Ancient Grove)

## Content

### Family Overview

The fae of the Ancient Grove are beautiful, capricious, and fundamentally unconcerned with your wellbeing. They are not evil (evil requires intent, and the fae's intent changes with the wind). They are simply operating on a set of rules that nobody else was consulted on, involving concepts like "trespass," "tribute," and "we were here first, so everything that happens to you is technically your fault." They live in the canopy, they throw things at visitors, and they consider the distinction between "game" and "violence" to be a matter of perspective. Theirs.

---

### Pixie Swarm
*Appearance:* A whirling cloud of tiny, luminous figures, each no larger than a dragonfly, trailing sparks of colour in their wake. Individually, they are almost charming: delicate wings, miniature features, expressions of intense concentration. Collectively, they are a headache in the most literal sense possible.
*Behavior:* Pixie swarms descend from the canopy in glittering waves, surrounding targets in a disorienting spiral of light and sound. Confusion on round 2 is not an attack so much as a side effect of having several dozen tiny creatures screaming at you simultaneously in a language that operates on frequencies your brain was not designed to process. Your accuracy drops. Your patience follows.
*Lore:* Pixies are the fae's children, or their pets, or their art projects. Nobody is sure, and asking a pixie results in an answer that takes forty minutes, involves three tangents, and concludes with the pixie forgetting the question. They are harmless individually. They are never individual.
*Combat Feel:* The highest evasion in the Ancient Grove (14) combined with the lowest HP (20). Confusion on round 2 drops your accuracy by 3, which stacks cruelly with their evasion to create a fight where you spend more time missing than hitting. Kill them fast (if you can hit them) or accept that this will take a while and cost you more stamina than it should.

### Thorn Fairy
*Appearance:* Larger than a pixie, roughly the size of a cat, with iridescent wings like stained glass and a body that looks deceptively delicate until you notice the thorns. They grow from its arms, its spine, and the edges of its wings in sharp, dark clusters that catch the grove's golden light like wet needles.
*Behavior:* Thorn fairies occupy the middle canopy, perching on branches and watching the forest floor with the detached interest of something that considers you a mild inconvenience. When provoked (which requires less than you would think), they fire Thorn Shot on round 3: a volley of barbed projectiles launched with the casual precision of someone who has been doing this for centuries and finds it neither exciting nor difficult.
*Lore:* Thorn fairies are the grove's gardeners in the same way that a cactus is a garden: technically accurate and deeply unwelcoming. They tend the Starbloom patches and the elderwood saplings, and they treat any non-fae presence near their charges as a personal affront. The thorns they shed are occasionally collected by foragers, who describe the process as "uncomfortable but profitable."
*Combat Feel:* Better balanced than the pixie swarm: decent evasion (10), moderate HP, and Thorn Shot that deals reliable magic damage. They are the fae family's workhorse, consistent and annoying without being overwhelming. The danger is in multiples: one thorn fairy is manageable, three are a problem, and they are rarely alone.

### Fae Knight
*Appearance:* Human-sized, armoured in plates of living bark inlaid with crystal, carrying a sword that shimmers with enchantment and wearing an expression of aristocratic contempt that suggests it has been practising in front of a mirror. Its wings are large, angular, and held folded behind its back like a cloak.
*Behavior:* Fae knights are the Canopy Court's border guard, patrolling the approaches to the Fae Queen's domain with the crisp discipline of soldiers serving a monarch they genuinely respect. Enchanted Blade on round 2 buffs their attack by 3 for three rounds, turning an already dangerous melee fighter into something considerably worse. Fae Strike on round 5 is a committed overhead blow channelling the enchantment into a single, devastating hit. They fight with the precise, formal aggression of someone who considers combat a courtly art.
*Lore:* Fae knights were not born; they were appointed. Each one was once an ordinary fae (pixie, thorn fairy, or something in between) that the Queen selected, elevated, and armoured through a ritual that nobody outside the Court has witnessed. The process changes them. They become taller, stronger, and significantly less whimsical, which in fae terms means they only throw things at you if you deserve it.
*Combat Feel:* The fae family's elite. Defence of 16 with evasion of 8 means they can take hits and dodge them. Enchanted Blade on round 2 creates a damage window (rounds 2-4) where their attack is significantly boosted, and Fae Strike on round 5 delivers the spike. The best strategy is to burst them down before round 2 completes or weather the buff phase and fight them at base stats after it expires. They are the first fae that demands real gear investment.

### Fae Queen
*Appearance:* Regal and terrifying in equal measure. Tall, slender, and composed of light and living wood, with a crown of crystal branches that refracts the grove's ambient glow into a halo of shifting colours. Her wings span the width of a clearing, translucent and patterned like stained glass windows depicting scenes that change depending on who is looking. Her expression is serene. It does not change, regardless of what she is doing. This is, depending on the circumstance, either reassuring or deeply alarming.
*Behavior:* The Fae Queen holds the Canopy Court and does not descend to the forest floor. You come to her, or more accurately, her knights bring you. Royal Guard on round 2 raises her defence by 4 for three rounds, because the Queen does not take damage from commoners without due preparation. Charm on round 4 reduces your attack by 4 for two rounds: not a spell, exactly, but an exertion of personality so absolute that your body decides, briefly, that fighting her is a bad idea. Fae Wrath on round 6 is what happens when the Queen's patience expires. It is magic damage, it is substantial, and it arrives with a serenity that makes the violence feel administrative rather than personal.
*Lore:* The Fae Queen has ruled the Canopy Court for longer than Millbrook has existed, and she will rule it after Millbrook is a memory. She is not the grove's oldest inhabitant (that distinction belongs to the Ancient Spirit), but she is its most politically complex, maintaining alliances with the treants, tolerances with the spirits, and an ongoing territorial disagreement with the dryads that has been described by Vesper Tain as "the most polite war in the Pocketrealm's history." The Fae Crystal Staff (I-05) was carved from crystals that grew in her Court. She is aware of this. She has opinions about it. The crystals were not given; they were taken, and the fae consider this an open account.
*Combat Feel:* The fae family's boss. 75 HP, defence of 20, and the highest magic defence in the game at her tier (26). Royal Guard (round 2) makes her nearly impervious for three rounds. Charm (round 4) cripples your damage output while you are trying to break through her defences. Fae Wrath (round 6) punishes anyone who has not solved the first two problems. The fight is a puzzle: survive the guard phase, endure the charm, and hit hard when the buffs expire. Physical damage is heavily recommended; her magic defence is a wall that most casters cannot breach. She fights like a queen: from a position of absolute advantage, and with the calm certainty that losing is not something that happens to her.
