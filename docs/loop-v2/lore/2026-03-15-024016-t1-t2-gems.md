# Item Descriptions: T1 + T2 Gems

## Content

### T1 Gems (Forest Edge gathering crits)

**Rough Ruby**
A cloudy red stone the size of a thumbnail, pried from a copper vein during a lucky swing. Uncut and unpolished, it still catches the light in a way that makes you forget you are standing in a ditch.

**Raw Amber**
A warm, honey-coloured bead found tangled in the roots of a Forest Sage patch. Something small and ancient is visible inside, perfectly preserved. Vesper says it is a beetle. You prefer not to look too closely.

**Tree Resin**
A hardened glob of golden sap that seeps from the oldest oaks when the wood is struck just right. It smells faintly of vanilla and stubbornly resists any attempt to dissolve it, which makes it useful for things that need to stay stuck.

### T2 Gems (Deep Forest / Cave Entrance gathering crits)

**Rough Sapphire**
A deep blue crystal, rough-edged and faintly translucent, pulled from a tin deposit in the Deep Forest. Rowan Delk calls finding one "the stone's way of saying thank you." He does not say this often, and never where anyone can hear him.

**Raw Pearl**
A pale, lustrous sphere found nestled among Moonpetal roots, formed over years in soil saturated with ambient magic. It is cool to the touch regardless of the temperature around it, which Vesper finds fascinating and everyone else finds slightly unnerving.

**Fossilized Sap**
Ancient tree sap, compressed and crystallized over centuries into a translucent amber stone harder than glass. Found in the heartwood of Deep Forest maples, usually only when the woodcutter's axe hits a pocket they were not expecting. The surprise is mutual.

## Integration Notes

- Add as `flavorText` on the `ItemTemplate` model, same pattern as all item entries.
- **Gathering crit context:** Gems drop randomly on critical gathering actions. The descriptions reference the specific gathering skill and zone where each appears: mining crits for rubies/sapphires (Copper Hollows Z-02, Tin Ridge Z-03), foraging crits for amber/pearl (Sage Patches Z-02, Moonpetal Glades Z-03), woodcutting crits for resin/sap (oaks Z-02, maples Z-03).
- **Cross-references:**
  - Raw Amber references Vesper Tain (N-04) identifying the preserved insect.
  - Rough Sapphire references Rowan Delk (N-06), showing his softer side (consistent with his gem idle line: "the gems come when they come").
  - Raw Pearl references Moonpetal (I-06 potion ingredients, Z-03 Moonpetal Glades).
  - Fossilized Sap references Deep Forest maples (Z-03 Maple Stands).
  - Tree Resin references the Forest Edge oaks (Z-02 Old Woodcutting Trail).
- **Tone:** Gems are rare, pleasant surprises in the middle of routine gathering. The descriptions are warmer and more wonder-touched than standard resource lore, reflecting the "lucky find" feeling of a gathering crit.
- **Cut gems:** These raw gems can be refined into cut versions (Cut Ruby, Cut Sapphire, etc.) for jewellery crafting. The raw descriptions focus on discovery; cut gem descriptions (future entry) will focus on refined beauty.
