# Item Descriptions: Jewellery

## Content

**Copper Ring**
A thin band of hammered copper, crudely polished and sold by Bram for a price that suggests he values the sentiment more than the material. It makes you slightly luckier, which in the Pocketrealm means the difference between a critical hit and a near miss.

**Copper Pendant**
A flat disc of copper on a leather cord, warm against the chest and inscribed with a mark that Bram says means "fortune" and Kessa says means "adequate."

**Iron Band**
Heavier than copper and twice as serious, forged from Deep Forest iron into a ring that sits on the finger like a small commitment to staying alive. The accuracy boost is slight but measurable, and Aldric would classify the luck improvement as "statistically significant, if barely."

**Dark Iron Ring**
Forged from Haunted Marsh dark iron, this ring carries a faint chill that has nothing to do with temperature and everything to do with where the ore was mined. Kessa works dark iron jewellery reluctantly, not because the material is difficult but because she considers jewellery a frivolous use of a serious metal.

**Mithril Ring**
A band of Crystal Caverns mithril so light you forget it is there and so precisely crafted that Kessa will not discuss how many attempts it took. The critical damage amplification is not magic; it is weight distribution channelling force into the exact point where it matters most.

**Ancient Ring**
Carved from Sunken Ruins ore into a band that is warm regardless of temperature and hums at a frequency Vesper can detect but not explain. The inscription on the inner surface is in a language that predates everything except the ruins it came from.

## Integration Notes

- Add as `flavorText` on the `ItemTemplate` model, keyed by the `IDS.jewel.*` item IDs.
- **Jewellery is the luck stat family.** All pieces provide luck as their primary stat, with rings adding crit/accuracy and pendants/necklaces adding health. The descriptions treat luck as a real, measurable force in the Pocketrealm rather than superstition, which grounds the stat in the world.
- **Ore tier progression:** The jewellery tiers mirror the ore progression established across all previous item entries: Copper (Forest Edge, T1), Iron (Deep Forest, T2), Dark Iron (Haunted Marsh, T3), Mithril (Crystal Caverns, T4), Ancient (Sunken Ruins, T5). Each description connects the ring to its source zone's atmosphere and materials.
- **Cross-references:**
  - Bram Holloway (N-01) anchors the T1 entries, selling copper jewellery with the same pragmatic warmth he brings to everything in the general store. The "fortune" vs. "adequate" disagreement with Kessa establishes their differing perspectives on the same material.
  - Kessa Ironweld (N-02) appears in four of six entries, her reactions escalating from dismissal (Copper Pendant: "adequate"), to reluctant craft (Dark Iron: "frivolous use of a serious metal"), to silent perfectionism (Mithril: will not discuss how many attempts). This is her longest cross-entry arc and mirrors the weapons progression.
  - Aldric Voss (N-05) referenced in the Iron Band with his statistical framing of luck, consistent with his ledger-and-data approach to everything.
  - Vesper Tain (N-04) anchors the Ancient Ring with her inability to explain the humming frequency, continuing her T5-tier pattern of encountering things beyond her analysis (Lich Staff, Eldritch Fragment, now the Ancient Ring).
  - The Ancient Ring's unreadable inscription connects to the Sunken Ruins' lost civilisation (Z-11), the Mysterious Stranger's carvings lore hook (N-12), and Aldric's compulsive cataloguing of Ancient Relics (I-14).
- **Tone progression:** Copper is humble and transactional. Iron is practical. Dark Iron is serious and slightly unsettling. Mithril is precise and understated. Ancient is reverent and mysterious. This mirrors the weapon tier arc (I-01 through I-09) but filtered through the intimacy of jewellery: these are small items worn close to the body, and the descriptions reflect that closeness.
- **Items not covered:** The tracker specifies six representative pieces (one per tier, plus the T1 pendant). The remaining jewellery (Copper Charm, Iron Chain, Iron Talisman, Dark Iron Amulet, Dark Iron Charm, Mithril Necklace, Mithril Talisman, Ancient Amulet, Ancient Charm) can receive flavor text in a future pass following the same tone-per-tier pattern.
