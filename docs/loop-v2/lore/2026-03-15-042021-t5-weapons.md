# Item Descriptions: T5 Weapons

## Content

**Mithril Blade**
A blade forged from Crystal Caverns mithril, lighter than iron and harder than anything Kessa Ironweld has ever worked. It holds an edge so fine that the air seems to part before the swing arrives. She made three of these before she was satisfied with one. She will not discuss what happened to the other two.

**Ancient Bow**
Carved from Sunken Ruins petrified wood that spent centuries hardening under salt water and arcane pressure. The grain has crystallised into something that is no longer wood and not quite stone. Arrows leave the string with a sound like tearing silk, and they arrive with a sound like nothing at all.

**Lich Staff**
A staff of petrified wood crowned with a crystal that pulses with a cold, pale light and does not stop pulsing, even when stored, even when buried, even when you are trying to sleep. The magical throughput is the highest of any craftable weapon in the Pocketrealm. Vesper Tain examined one and went very quiet for a long time before saying, simply, "Be careful with this." She did not elaborate. She did not need to.

## Integration Notes

- Add as `flavorText` on the `ItemTemplate` model, same pattern as all weapon entries.
- **Endgame pinnacle:** T5 weapons are the final crafted tier. Only three weapons, no mob-material variants. The descriptions treat these as singular, culminating achievements rather than incremental upgrades.
- **Cross-references:**
  - Mithril Blade references Kessa Ironweld (N-02) and the Crystal Caverns mithril ore. The detail about three attempts and two failures adds depth to Kessa's perfectionism and suggests the material is genuinely difficult to work.
  - Ancient Bow references the Sunken Ruins and petrified wood (T5 woodcutting resource). The crystallisation detail connects to the zone's underwater arcane environment.
  - Lich Staff references Vesper Tain (N-04) and petrified wood. Vesper's uncharacteristic silence (she usually talks too much) signals that this weapon is in a different category from anything she has studied before.
- **Tone escalation across tiers:** T1 was humble and self-deprecating. T2 was a genuine step up. T3 carried authority. T4 was dangerous and unsettling. T5 is reverent. These weapons are not funny. They are not comforting. They are the best things mortal hands can make, and the descriptions treat them with the weight that deserves.
- **NPC reactions as barometers:** Kessa's perfectionism (three attempts), the Ancient Bow's silence (beyond sound), and Vesper's quiet (beyond analysis) all signal endgame gravitas through characters the player has known since Millbrook.
- **No stat explanations:** Unlike previous tiers, T5 descriptions do not explain their stats mechanically. At this level, the weapon speaks for itself. The flavor is about the experience of holding something extraordinary, not the numbers behind it.
