# NPC Dialogue: Town Guard (Millbrook Gate)

## Content

### Character Brief

**Name:** Captain Fen Darrow
**Location:** Millbrook's gate, leaning against the palisade
**Personality:** Tired, observant, and doing the best job she can with the budget she has been given, which is not enough. Fen has been captain of Millbrook's three-person guard rotation for six years, and in that time she has defended the town against exactly one Great Boar incursion, two bandit raids, and an uncountable number of rats that do not respect palisade engineering. She speaks with the flat, measured patience of someone who has explained the same safety protocols to the same people who will ignore them for the same reasons. She is not bitter. She is realistic, which in Millbrook looks similar.

---

### Greeting Lines (on interaction)

> "Heading out? Stay on the paths if you can. Off the paths if something is on them. And if you see a Rat King, do not try to negotiate. We tried that. It did not go well."

> "Welcome back. Still breathing, still upright, still have all your limbs. That puts you in the top half of today's returnees. Congratulations."

> "The gate's open until sundown. After that, knock three times and wait. If I do not answer, I am either on patrol or asleep. Both are equally likely."

### Idle / Atmosphere Lines (while interacting)

> "See that dent in the gate? Great Boar, two winters ago. Came right up the road like it owned the place. We reinforced the timbers after that. The boar has not come back. Coincidence? Possibly. I choose to believe in the timbers."

> "I keep a list of known bandit operatives. Aldric Voss keeps a better one. We compare notes on Thursdays. His handwriting is neater. My information is more recent. We consider this a fair exchange."

> "Rowan is out there somewhere, leaning against the other side of this wall. We do not talk much. He watches the forest. I watch the gate. Between us, we cover the important angles."

> "Maren tells me everything she hears at the Antler, which is more intelligence than my entire budget provides. I buy her a drink once a month as compensation. Millbrook's defence strategy runs on cheap ale and goodwill. It is more effective than it has any right to be."

### Warning Lines (zone-related, on heading to dangerous zones)

> "The Forest Edge? Manageable. Keep your weapon drawn and your back to something solid. The things out there are small and stupid. Mostly. The mostly is what gets people."

> "Deep Forest? Watch for bandits on the Old Road. They favour the narrow stretches. And if the trees start moving, that is not your imagination. That is your cue to leave."

> "You are heading past the plains? To Thornwall? Good luck. I mean that. Gavrik runs a tight operation out there, but the things between here and there do not care about operations."

### Farewell Lines (on leaving)

> "Stay sharp. Come back. In that order."

> "The gate will be here when you return. So will I. That is the entirety of my job description, and I take it seriously."

## Integration Notes

- **Same dialogue system as all previous NPCs:** Keyed by NPC ID and event type, random selection. New event type: "zone_warning" triggered when the player is heading to a specific zone, with lines varying by destination.
- **Character name (Captain Fen Darrow):** Eleventh named NPC. Seventh Millbrook NPC. She is the guard captain referenced in the bandit bestiary (M-05: "Millbrook's guard captain keeps a list").
- **Cross-references:**
  - The Gate dent pays off the Great Boar lore (M-03: "Millbrook's gate was reinforced three times") and the gate description (Z-01: "sturdy enough to discourage anything up to the size of a large boar").
  - Aldric Voss (N-05) is referenced as a fellow list-keeper, establishing their Thursday intelligence-sharing routine.
  - Rowan Delk (N-06) is referenced as her counterpart on the other side of the wall, creating a spatial relationship: Fen inside, Rowan outside.
  - Maren Ashwick (N-03) is referenced as an intelligence source through tavern gossip, connecting the guard to the community network.
  - Gavrik Stoneshoulder (N-08) is referenced in the Thornwall warning, bridging the two settlements.
  - Zone warnings reference Forest Edge (Z-02), Deep Forest (Z-03, bandits and treants), and the Whispering Plains route to Thornwall (Z-07).
- **Personality niche:** Among Millbrook NPCs: Bram (commerce), Kessa (craft), Maren (community), Vesper (knowledge), Aldric (order), Rowan (the land), Fen (protection). She is the town's thin line between safety and wilderness, and her dialogue reflects both competence and resource constraints.
- **Three-person guard:** Mentioned in the character brief. This establishes that Millbrook's defence is minimal, which explains why adventurers are necessary and why the town relies on NPC networks (Maren's gossip, Aldric's records, Rowan's observation) rather than military strength.
