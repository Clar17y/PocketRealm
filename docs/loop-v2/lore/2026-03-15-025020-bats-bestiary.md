# Mob Flavor Text: Bats (Cave Entrance)

## Content

### Family Overview

The bats of the Cave Entrance are the underground's answer to a question nobody asked: what if something could see you perfectly in total darkness, fly faster than you can swing, and had absolutely no interest in a fair fight? They roost in the high chambers where stalactites cluster like inverted forests, and they descend in waves that are less "attack" and more "weather event." Hitting one is an achievement. Hitting one that matters is an education.

---

### Cave Bat
*Appearance:* About the size of a crow, with leathery wings stretched taut over delicate bones and a face that evolution designed for echolocation and nothing else. Its fur is dark grey, its eyes are vestigial pinpricks, and its mouth is a pink, shrieking circle of needle teeth.
*Behavior:* Cave bats hang in clusters from the upper passages, dropping into flight when something warm enters their airspace. They attack in swooping arcs, darting in to bite and wheeling away before you can respond. Individually, they are a nuisance. In numbers, they are a legitimate problem, and they are never alone.
*Lore:* The guano they leave behind is, according to Vesper Tain, "one of the most underappreciated alchemical substrates in the Pocketrealm." According to everyone who has to walk through it, it is something else entirely.
*Combat Feel:* High evasion, low everything else. Cave bats are the first enemies that teach you accuracy matters. You will miss more than you hit, and each miss costs stamina you cannot afford to waste. Fast fights if you connect; frustrating ones if you do not.

### Dire Bat
*Appearance:* Twice the size of a common cave bat, with a wingspan that fills a tunnel from wall to wall. Its fur is jet black, its ears are enormous and constantly swivelling, and its claws are long enough to leave marks in stone. It does not look like something that should be able to fly. It flies anyway, with unsettling grace.
*Behavior:* Dire bats are solitary hunters that sweep through the open galleries in slow, silent arcs, using echolocation to map prey from distances that would make a surface predator jealous. They strike from above, raking with claws before climbing back out of reach. They do not screech before attacking. The silence is the warning.
*Lore:* The bat wing membrane used in crossbow construction comes primarily from dire bats, whose wings are larger, more elastic, and structurally superior to their smaller cousins'. Harvesting them requires killing the bat first, which requires hitting the bat first, which is the part that makes the crossbows expensive.
*Combat Feel:* Slightly less evasive than the cave bat (evasion 7 vs 8) but tougher in every other way. More HP, better accuracy, and damage that starts to hurt. The silent approach means no warning; dire bat fights tend to start with you already being hit.

### Vampire Bat
*Appearance:* Sleek, dark-furred, and wrong. Its eyes glow a faint, dull red in torchlight, and its fangs are longer than a bat has any right to possess. The membrane of its wings has a translucent quality, and if you look closely (which you should not), you can see the veins pulsing beneath the skin.
*Behavior:* Vampire bats do not hunt for food in the conventional sense. They hunt to sustain something that food alone cannot. Life Drain on round 3 is not just an attack; it is a transfer, pulling vitality from its target and converting it directly into healing. A vampire bat that is allowed to drain will outlast fighters with twice its stats. They prefer extended engagements for exactly this reason, circling and stalling until the drain does its work.
*Lore:* Vesper Tain has studied vampire bat physiology extensively and concluded that Life Drain is not magic, exactly. It is something older: a biological process that the Pocketrealm's ambient energy has accelerated into something that works faster than nature intended. She finds this "profoundly interesting." Her neighbours find it profoundly disturbing that she keeps samples.
*Combat Feel:* The bat family's mid-boss. Highest evasion in the family (9), combined with Life Drain on round 3 that heals for as much as it damages. The fight becomes a race: kill it before the drain cycle turns the engagement in its favour. High burst damage is the answer. Sustained fights are what it wants.

### Bat Swarm Lord
*Appearance:* Not a single bat but hundreds, moving as one body. A churning, screeching cloud of dark wings and red eyes that fills the upper half of whatever cavern it occupies. At its centre, barely visible, something larger holds the swarm together: a dire bat of unusual size whose echolocation pulses coordinate the colony like a heartbeat.
*Behavior:* The Bat Swarm Lord opens with Screech on round 2, a concentrated sonic pulse that rattles your skull and reduces your accuracy for two rounds (a bat that makes you worse at hitting bats is, frankly, unfair game design and excellent natural selection). On round 5, it releases the Swarm: the colony descends in a coordinated dive that hits like a wall of leather and teeth. Between these abilities, it fights conventionally, which for something made of several hundred angry bats is still quite bad.
*Lore:* The largest bat colony in the Cave Entrance roosts in a chamber so deep that torchlight cannot reach the ceiling. The Swarm Lord has claimed this space for decades, growing its colony through a combination of territorial aggression and the simple mathematics of having more bats than anything else has reasons to argue. Maren Ashwick says a traveller once described seeing the Swarm Lord in flight as "looking up and realizing the ceiling was moving." He did not stay to elaborate.
*Combat Feel:* The bat family's boss. Screech on round 2 cripples your accuracy right when you need it most, and Swarm on round 5 delivers a burst of damage that punishes anyone who has not dealt with the accuracy debuff. The strategy is to weather the Screech, recover, and kill it before the next cycle. Evasion drops to 6 (the swarm is large, not agile), but the HP pool and ability timing make it a genuine challenge. Bring accuracy buffs or accept that the first three rounds will be ugly.

- Bat family site nouns (Roost, Colony, Cavern) map naturally: cave bats in roosts, colonies forming around dire bats, the Swarm Lord's cavern.
- **Design theme:** All bats share high evasion as their defining trait, narratively explained through flight, echolocation, and aerial mobility. The Swarm Lord's lower evasion (6) is explained by the swarm's size.
