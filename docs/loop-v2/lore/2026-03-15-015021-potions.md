# Item Descriptions: Potions

## Content

**Minor Health Potion**
Forest Sage extract, distilled twice and stabilized with a binding agent Vesper refuses to name. Tastes like warm copper and bad decisions, but it closes wounds faster than rest and cheaper than prayer.

**Health Potion**
A deeper red than its minor cousin, brewed from Moonpetal and a base that takes three days to prepare. The healing is immediate, thorough, and accompanied by a brief tingling sensation that Vesper insists is normal.

**Cleansing Potion**
Three years of failed antivenom research condensed into a bottle that actually works (on everything except, infuriatingly, common spider venom). Strips poison, curses, and lingering magical effects with the gentle subtlety of a cold river.

**Greater Health Potion**
Starbloom essence suspended in a solution so precisely balanced that Vesper labels each batch personally. Heals deep wounds, knits bone, and temporarily makes you feel invincible, which is the dangerous part.

**Resist Potion**
A thick, iridescent liquid brewed from Gravemoss and dark iron filings. Hardens your body against incoming damage for several rounds. The aftertaste is indescribable, in the sense that Vesper has described it and nobody wants to hear it twice.

**Mana Potion**
Shimmer Fern extract rendered into a luminous blue draught that restores spent magical energy. Mages report a sensation of "cold clarity" after drinking it. Vesper reports that they also tend to talk too much for the next hour.

**Elixir of Power**
Vesper's masterwork: Abyssal Kelp distilled with methods she will not teach and reagents she will not source publicly. Amplifies your damage output significantly for the duration. The bottle is warm to the touch, which is concerning, but the results speak louder than the concerns.

## Integration Notes

- Add as `flavorText` on the `ItemTemplate` model, same pattern as all item entries.
- **Vesper Tain (N-04)** is referenced throughout as the brewer. This is consistent with her character brief and dialogue, and grounds the potion economy in a specific person rather than abstract crafting.
- **Ingredient references:** Minor Health uses Forest Sage (I-02). Health uses Moonpetal (Deep Forest T2 resource). Greater Health uses Starbloom (Ancient Grove T3 resource). Resist uses Gravemoss (Haunted Marsh T4 resource). Mana uses Shimmer Fern (Crystal Caverns T4 resource). Elixir uses Abyssal Kelp (Sunken Ruins T5 resource). Each potion tier is tied to a specific zone's foraging resource, creating a clear progression path.
- **Cleansing Potion** pays off the running antivenom joke from M-02 (spiders bestiary) and N-04 (Vesper's idle line about the umbrella that works in every weather except rain). The flavor notes that it works on everything except common spider venom, which is a deliberate callback.
- **Potion tier progression:** The descriptions grow more reverent and more dangerous as the tier increases, mirroring the ingredient rarity and the zones they come from. Minor Health is casual. Elixir of Power is handled with respect.
- **Display context:** These descriptions work in inventory tooltips, the potion crafting results screen, and Vesper's shop when browsing purchasable potions.
