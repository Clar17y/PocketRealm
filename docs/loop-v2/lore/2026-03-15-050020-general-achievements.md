# Achievement Flavor: General Milestones

## Content

### Turns Spent Track

#### Time Invested (10,000 turns spent)

*"Ten thousand turns. That is roughly three hours of real time, give or take, spent doing things that a sensible person would avoid: fighting rats, mining copper, and arguing with the forest about whether its trees are allowed to move. You are not a sensible person. The Pocketrealm appreciates that."*

Unlock text: Ten thousand turns. You have stopped being a tourist and started being a resident.

#### Dedicated Adventurer (100,000 turns spent, title: The Dedicated)

*"One hundred thousand turns. Days of real time spent in the Pocketrealm, measured in swings, steps, and the slow accumulation of experience that turns a wanderer into a fixture. Bram has memorised your order. Maren has saved your seat. Aldric has stopped double-checking your paperwork. You belong here now, in the quiet, permanent way that only time can establish."*

Unlock text: A hundred thousand turns, spent willingly. Dedication is not a burst of effort. It is the decision to keep showing up.

#### No-Lifer (1,000,000 turns spent, title: The No-Lifer, +2 attribute points)

*"One million turns. The number is absurd and the title is earned. You have fought in every zone, gathered from every node, crafted at every bench, and spent more time in the Pocketrealm than some of its permanent residents. Lira Caravel considers you a supply chain. Rowan Delk considers you a colleague. Gavrik Stoneshoulder considers you proof that individual stubbornness can, given enough time, reshape the world. He is not wrong."*

Unlock text: A million turns. The No-Lifer. It sounds like an insult but wears like a crown. You have given this world more time than it had any right to ask for, and it gave you a story worth the investment.

### Recipe Track

#### Cookbook (10 recipes learned)

*"Ten recipes committed to memory. Swords, armour, potions, and at least one thing you made by accident that turned out better than anything you made on purpose. The Crafting Quarter is starting to feel like a second home, and Kessa has stopped hovering when you use the forge."*

Unlock text: Ten recipes. Your crafting repertoire has officially outgrown "beginner" and entered "competent." The smoke stains on your clothes confirm it.

#### Recipe Collector (25 recipes learned, title: The Collector, +1 attribute point)

*"Twenty-five recipes. You can make weapons from five different metals, brew potions from herbs that most people cannot pronounce, and tan leather that even the bog refuses to rot. The knowledge is not written down anywhere complete; it lives in your hands, in the muscle memory of a hundred crafting sessions, in the instinct that tells you when the iron is ready by the colour of the flame rather than the temperature."*

Unlock text: Twenty-five recipes, collected one by one. The Collector. You do not just know how to make things. You know how to make nearly everything.

## Integration Notes

- **Same display pattern as all previous achievement entries:** Short unlock text as toast/popup; italic passage in achievement detail view.
- **Two tracks, two themes:** Turns spent achievements reward persistence and presence (being here). Recipe achievements reward breadth of crafting knowledge (knowing how). Both are "general" category because they measure investment across all activities rather than specialising in one.
- **Cross-references:**
  - Time Invested references the core early-game loop (rats, copper, moving trees) as a callback to M-01, I-02, and M-06.
  - Dedicated Adventurer references Bram (N-01), Maren (N-03), and Aldric (N-05) recognising the player as a regular, making the milestone feel communal.
  - No-Lifer references Lira Caravel (N-09), Rowan Delk (N-06), and Gavrik Stoneshoulder (N-08), drawing from the advanced NPCs to match the advanced milestone. Each NPC's reaction is in character: Lira sees supply, Rowan sees a peer, Gavrik sees proof.
  - Cookbook references Kessa Ironweld (N-02) and the Crafting Quarter (Z-01).
  - Recipe Collector references materials from multiple zones (five metals, herbs, bog leather) to emphasise breadth.
- **Turn conversion context:** 10,000 turns is roughly 2.8 hours of real time at 1 turn/second regen. The "roughly three hours" line is deliberately approximate and conversational, grounding the abstract number in something tangible.
- **Title comedy:** "The No-Lifer" is self-aware about being a backhanded compliment. The flavor text acknowledges this directly ("sounds like an insult but wears like a crown"), turning what could be a mean joke into a badge of genuine pride.
