# Zone Atmosphere: Millbrook

## Content

### Arrival Text

The road narrows, the trees thin, and there it is: Millbrook. A cluster of timber-framed buildings at the point where farmland meets forest, smelling of woodsmoke and fresh bread and just a little bit of manure. It is not much. But it is yours, for now.

### Ambient Descriptions

*Morning:*
Sunlight slants across the cobblestones as shutters bang open along the main street. A blacksmith's hammer rings out in steady rhythm. Somewhere, a rooster crows for the third time, and someone throws a boot at it.

*Afternoon:*
The market square hums with low commerce. Farmers argue over grain prices. A pair of apprentice crafters compare burn marks with quiet pride. The smell of the tavern's midday stew drifts through every open door, persistent as a rumor.

*Evening:*
Lanterns flicker to life along the main road. The tavern fills. Returning adventurers swap stories that grow taller with each telling, while merchants count the day's take and the town guard leans against the gate, watching the treeline.

*Night:*
Millbrook goes quiet, but never quite silent. Crickets fill the dark. The forge's coals glow dull orange through the smithy window. Somewhere past the gate, something shrieks in the Forest Edge, and no one looks up from their drink.

### Environmental Flavor

**The Main Street:** A dirt road paved with ambition and about twelve yards of actual cobblestone. Stalls line both sides: general goods to the left, a weapons rack to the right, and the ever-present smell of tanning leather from the workshop out back.

**The Town Square:** A modest clearing with a well at its center, perpetually surrounded by people who are definitely about to get water and not just standing around gossiping. A cork notice board by the well posts bounties, warnings, and one very persistent advertisement for singing lessons.

**The Crafting Quarter:** Three workshops crowd together behind the tavern. The forge, the tannery, and an alchemist's bench that occasionally emits sounds that make the neighbors nervous. Apprentices are welcome. Explosions are not, but they happen anyway.

**The Gate:** A wooden palisade with a single gate facing the Forest Edge. It looks sturdy enough to discourage anything up to the size of a large boar. Anything bigger, and the town's defence strategy shifts to "hope and distance." A guard is usually posted here. Usually.

**The Tavern (The Crooked Antler):** The oldest building in Millbrook, identifiable by its sagging roofline and the antler mounted above the door that hasn't hung straight since anyone can remember. Inside: warm light, cheap ale, and the comfortable noise of people pretending the wilderness beyond the walls is someone else's problem.
