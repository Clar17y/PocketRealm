# Achievement Flavor: Crafting Milestones

## Content

### Craft Count Track

#### Apprentice (1 item crafted)

*"Your first creation. It is not perfect. Kessa would call it 'functional,' which from her is practically a standing ovation. But it is yours, made by your hands from materials you gathered, and it exists because you decided it should. That counts for something."*

Unlock text: You made a thing. It did not explode. This is how every great smith, alchemist, and cook in history started.

#### Artisan (50 items crafted, title: The Artisan)

*"Fifty items. Your hands have learned the rhythms of the forge, the bench, and the tanning rack without your mind needing to instruct them. You no longer burn yourself on hot ingots (usually), and the quality of your work has moved from 'surprising' to 'expected.' In the Crafting Quarter, that is the highest compliment."*

Unlock text: Fifty crafted, each one better than the last. Or at least different from the last. Growth takes many forms.

#### Master Crafter (500 items crafted, title: Master Crafter, +2 attribute points)

*"Five hundred. The raw materials of the Pocketrealm have passed through your hands so many times that you can identify ore by weight, wood by smell, and leather by the way it resists the needle. Kessa Ironweld has stopped checking your work. Vesper Tain has started asking for your opinion on compound stability. You have not merely learned a trade; you have become the standard against which the trade is measured."*

Unlock text: Five hundred items forged, stitched, brewed, and shaped. The materials know your hands. The craft knows your name.

### Rarity Track

#### Lucky Break (1 rare item crafted)

*"The hammer fell, the metal sang, and something clicked into place that had no business clicking. A rare item, born from the same materials as a dozen common ones before it, distinguished by nothing except the inexplicable alchemy of the right force at the right angle at the right moment. Vesper would call it chemistry. Kessa would call it instinct. You are not sure what to call it, but you would like it to happen again."*

Unlock text: Your first rare craft. Luck or skill? Probably luck. But do not let that stop you from taking full credit.

#### Epic Forger (1 epic item crafted, title: The Forger)

*"Epic. The word gets thrown around too freely in tavern stories, but not here. What you made is genuinely, objectively exceptional: a piece of equipment that will outlast its owner, outperform its peers, and make Kessa Ironweld give it a long, silent look that might be jealousy and might be respect. She will not tell you which."*

Unlock text: You have forged something that transcends the ordinary. The purple glow is not decoration. It is a promise.

#### Legendary Smith (1 legendary item crafted, title: Legendsmith, +1 attribute point)

*"There it is. A legendary item, crafted by mortal hands from mortal materials, yet somehow more than the sum of its parts. The grain of the metal catches light that is not there. The edge holds despite all physical reason. Aldric Voss will want to log it. Maren Ashwick will want to display it above the bar. Kessa Ironweld will say nothing at all, which is how you will know that what you have made is real."*

Unlock text: Legendary. Not found in a ruin or stripped from a fallen god. Made. By you. At a workbench. Sometimes the extraordinary begins with ordinary hands.

## Integration Notes

- **Same display pattern as previous achievement entries:** Short unlock text as toast/popup; italic expanded passage in achievement detail view.
- **Two tracks, two tones:** Craft-count achievements (Apprentice through Master Crafter) follow a progression of competence: clumsy beginnings, growing confidence, recognized mastery. Rarity achievements (Lucky Break through Legendary Smith) are about the unpredictable magic of creation: luck, transcendence, and the moment when materials exceed expectations.
- **Cross-references:**
  - Kessa Ironweld (N-02) is referenced in Apprentice, Master Crafter, Epic Forger, and Legendary Smith. She is the game's standard-bearer for craft, so her reactions serve as a narrative barometer for quality.
  - Vesper Tain (N-04) is referenced in Master Crafter (asking for opinions) and Lucky Break (calling it chemistry).
  - Aldric Voss (N-05) is referenced in Legendary Smith (wanting to log it).
  - Maren Ashwick (N-03) is referenced in Legendary Smith (wanting to display it).
  - The Crafting Quarter (Z-01) is referenced in Artisan.
- **NPC reaction pattern:** The Legendary Smith entry uses all four core Millbrook NPCs reacting differently to the same event, which reinforces their distinct personalities and makes the achievement feel like a community moment rather than a solo one.
- **Tone:** Crafting achievements are warmer and more reflective than combat or exploration. The humour is gentler, rooted in the satisfaction of making things rather than destroying them.
