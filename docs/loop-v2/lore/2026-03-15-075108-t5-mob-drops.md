# Item Descriptions: T5 Mob Drops

## Content

**Naga Scale**
A broad, iridescent plate shed by a Sunken Ruins naga, dense as stone and slick with a salt-mineral coating that never fully dries. Kessa works them into armour with a reverence she reserves for materials that are older than the craft she uses to shape them.

**Spectral Silk**
A luminous thread drawn from the dissolution of a spectral captain: the fabric of a uniform that outlasted the body, the rank, and the civilisation that issued it. It is cold to the touch and faintly transparent, and it carries the outline of military insignia that Aldric Voss has no record of.

**Ooze Residue**
A viscous, faintly acidic gel collected from the remains of a Sunken Ruins ooze, stored in sealed glass because it dissolves everything else. Vesper uses it in concentrated alchemical preparations and says it is, chemically speaking, the most aggressive solvent in the Pocketrealm. She says this with admiration.

**Eldritch Fragment**
A shard of something that is not stone, not crystal, and not any material that has a name. It was recovered from the deepest chamber of the Sunken Ruins, where the Eldritch Abomination nests, and it pulses with a light that does not illuminate. Vesper studied one for three days and returned it without comment. She has not asked for another.

**Lich Dust**
A fine, grey powder collected from the space a Lich occupied before it stopped occupying it. The dust is what remains when a will that has sustained itself for centuries finally loses coherence: part ash, part magic, part the residue of a personality that refused to end. It is extraordinarily potent in necromantic applications. Nobody asks where it comes from more than once.

**Naga Pearl**
A deep-water pearl harvested from the Naga Queen's hoard, formed in the flooded galleries over centuries of mineral accretion around a core that Vesper believes is a fragment of Ancient Ore. If she is right, the pearl is the only known material where the ruins' ore and the ruins' water have fused into something neither could produce alone. It is beautiful. That is the least interesting thing about it.

**Ancient Relic**
A fragment of carved stone, metal, or ceramic from the civilisation that built the Sunken Ruins, recovered from the deepest and most intact chambers. Each relic is unique: a piece of a frieze, a section of inscription, a shard of a mechanism with no identifiable purpose. Aldric Voss catalogues them obsessively. He has filled four ledgers. He cannot read a single inscription. He keeps cataloguing.

## Integration Notes

- Add as `flavorText` on the `ItemTemplate` model, same pattern as I-04 (T1), I-11 (T2), I-12 (T3), I-13 (T4).
- **Source connections:** All seven drops are from Sunken Ruins (Z-11) families: Naga Scale and Naga Pearl from serpents (M-20 upcoming), Spectral Silk and Lich Dust from undead (M-21 upcoming), Ooze Residue and Eldritch Fragment from abominations (M-22 upcoming), Ancient Relic as a general Sunken Ruins drop.
- **Cross-references:**
  - Naga Scale references Kessa Ironweld (N-02) with the same reverence pattern established in T5 weapons (I-09). The scale becomes T5 medium armour (Naga Scale Armour), completing the material-to-gear pipeline.
  - Spectral Silk references Aldric Voss (N-05) and the lost civilisation's military. The insignia he cannot identify connects to the Throne Hall carvings (Z-11) and the Mysterious Stranger's lore about a civilisation that wrote its history on walls because nobody would be left to remember (N-12).
  - Ooze Residue references Vesper Tain (N-04) with her characteristic admiration for dangerous things. This is her third T5-tier reaction (after Lich Staff silence in I-09 and Abyssal Kelp description in Z-11).
  - Eldritch Fragment references Vesper again, but this time she returns the sample without comment. Where the Lich Staff made her say "be careful," the Fragment makes her say nothing. The escalation is deliberate.
  - Lich Dust stands alone without NPC reference. The description is self-contained because the Lich is the Sunken Ruins' most personal enemy: a will that refused to end. The material reflects that.
  - Naga Pearl references Vesper's theory about Ancient Ore fusion, connecting the serpent family to the ruins' ore deposits (Z-11 environmental flavor) and advancing the worldbuilding around the lost civilisation's relationship with the ore.
  - Ancient Relic references Aldric Voss (N-05) cataloguing what he cannot understand, echoing his role as the Pocketrealm's archivist and deepening the lost civilisation mystery.
- **T5 tone:** These drops complete the tier progression: T1 was practical and humble, T2 carried a hint of wildness, T3 brought beauty and strangeness, T4 was unsettling and partially alive. T5 is reverent and final. These materials come from the endgame zone, and they carry the weight of something ancient, irreducible, and not entirely understood. The descriptions do not explain what these materials are for. At T5, the material speaks for itself.
- **Vesper escalation:** Vesper Tain's reactions across T5 content form a deliberate arc: clinical admiration (Ooze Residue), quiet caution (Lich Staff in I-09), and silent refusal (Eldritch Fragment). Each response tells the player more about the material than any stat could.
