# Zone Atmosphere: Forest Edge

## Content

### Arrival Text

The gate closes behind you with a thud that feels more final than it should. Ahead, the forest begins: dappled sunlight, birdsong, and the faint rustle of things that have been watching you since you stepped outside. The path is well-worn. That is either reassuring or a warning, depending on your disposition.

### Ambient Descriptions

*Exploration (early):*
Sunlight filters through the oak canopy in shifting columns. The undergrowth is thick but navigable, and the air smells of damp earth and crushed herbs. Something chittered in the bushes a moment ago. It has gone quiet now, which is arguably worse.

*Exploration (mid):*
The trees grow closer together here, and the paths fork without signage. Spider silk catches the light between branches. Copper-streaked stones jut from the hillside where the soil has eroded. A boar snorts somewhere to your left, unseen but very much present.

*Exploration (deep):*
The canopy thickens overhead. Less sunlight reaches the forest floor, and the undergrowth gives way to moss and root. Webs stretch between the lower trunks in sheets. The chittering has been replaced by a heavier sound: something large, shifting its weight in the shadows ahead.

*After combat:*
The forest settles back into its rhythms quickly. Birds resume singing. Insects hum. Whatever you just killed will be scavenged before nightfall. The Forest Edge wastes nothing.

### Environmental Flavor

**The Treeline:** Where farmland ends and the forest begins. From Millbrook's gate, you can see exactly where the neat rows of crops give way to wild undergrowth. Farmers maintain a cleared strip between their fields and the trees. Nothing grows there. Nothing is allowed to.

**Copper Hollows:** A stretch of shallow ravines where copper ore veins run close to the surface. The soil here has a greenish tint, and the stones leave rusty smudges on your hands. Miners from Millbrook work these deposits in shifts, always in pairs, always watching the treeline.

**The Old Woodcutting Trail:** A muddy track winding through the oaks, marked by fresh stumps and the drag marks of hauled logs. Woodcutters have been working this trail for generations. The trees keep growing back, thicker each time, as if the forest is trying to make a point.

**Silk Hollows:** A low-lying area where the spiders are thickest. Webs hang between every trunk like wet laundry, and the air has a faintly sweet, chemical smell. Weavers from Millbrook come here to harvest silk with long poles and short tempers. The spiders tolerate this about as well as you would expect.

**The Sage Patches:** Scattered clearings where Forest Sage grows in dense, knee-high clusters. The herb's peppery scent keeps most vermin at bay, which makes these clearings some of the only places in the Forest Edge where you can sit down without checking for rats first. Foragers guard their favourite patches jealously.

### Exploration Tier Flavor

*Tier 1 (0-25%):* The well-trodden paths near Millbrook. You can still see the gate if you look back. The creatures here are small, common, and only mildly inconvenient.

*Tier 2 (25-50%):* Deeper in. The paths narrow and the undergrowth thickens. Larger creatures stake out territory here. The gate is no longer visible, and the forest has stopped feeling polite.

*Tier 3 (50-75%):* The old growth. Trees the width of carts, roots like walls, and silence where there should be birdsong. This is where the Rat Kings nest and the Brood Mothers weave. You came looking for a fight. You found one.

*Tier 4 (75-100%):* The heart of the Forest Edge, where the canopy blocks out the sky entirely. The Great Boar charges through the dark here. Whatever else lives this deep has learned to avoid it, which should tell you something about your chances.

## Integration Notes

- **Arrival text:** Display on first entry to Forest Edge from Millbrook. The gate reference connects directly to the Millbrook zone lore (Z-01).
- **Ambient descriptions:** Rotate in the exploration feed or idle state. The four variants correspond to early/mid/deep exploration progress and post-combat.
- **Environmental flavor:** Display as sub-location descriptions in a zone detail panel or "look around" feature. Each ties to a game system: Copper Hollows (mining), Old Woodcutting Trail (woodcutting), Sage Patches (foraging), Silk Hollows (spider encounters/Spider Silk drops).
- **Exploration tier flavor:** Map to the zone's `explorationTiers` config (`{ '1': 0, '2': 25, '3': 50, '4': 75 }`). Show as a brief descriptor when the player reaches a new tier threshold.
- **Cross-references:** Miners, woodcutters, weavers, and foragers from Millbrook are mentioned, consistent with established town lore. Rat Kings (M-01), Brood Mothers (M-02), Great Boar (future M-03), and Forest Sage (I-02) are all referenced naturally.
