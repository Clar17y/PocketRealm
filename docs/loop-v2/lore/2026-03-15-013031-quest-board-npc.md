# NPC Dialogue: Quest Board / Quest-Giver (Millbrook)

## Content

### Character Brief

**Name:** Aldric Voss
**Location:** The Town Square, beside the cork notice board near the well
**Personality:** Methodical, perpetually exasperated, and quietly proud of a job nobody thanks him for. Aldric is Millbrook's official bounty clerk: the man who collects reports, tallies kills, verifies claims, and distributes quest tokens with the grim efficiency of someone who has learned that adventurers will lie about absolutely anything if it means getting paid faster. He carries a ledger the size of a small shield, writes in ink that he mixes himself, and treats heroism as an administrative category rather than a moral one.

---

### Greeting Lines (on quest board open)

> "Ah, there you are. The board has been updated since this morning. Three new dailies, one weekly, and something that Bram insists is 'urgent' but which I suspect is just another rat problem. Have a look."

> "Quests. Bounties. Tasks. Whatever you want to call them, I have them, they need doing, and you need tokens. Let us proceed with mutual benefit and minimal small talk."

> "Good, a capable one. I can tell by the fact that you are standing upright and not bleeding. The board is to your left. Pick what suits you. I will handle the paperwork."

### Browse / Idle Lines (while quest panel is open)

> "Every quest on that board has been verified, categorized, and assigned a fair token reward. The system works. Slowly, and with complaints from all parties, but it works."

> "The singing lessons advertisement? Not mine. Someone keeps pinning it back up every time I remove it. I have my suspicions, but Maren denies everything, and she does it with a straight face, which I find deeply suspicious."

> "Weekly bounties pay better, but they require commitment. Daily tasks are smaller, faster, and less likely to get you killed. Choose according to your confidence and your schedule."

> "I had an adventurer last week claim they killed sixty monsters in a single day. Sixty. I asked for evidence. They showed me a sack of rat tails. I counted thirty-two. We had a conversation about arithmetic."

### Quest Accept Lines (on quest acceptance)

> "Noted. Tracked. The board knows what you owe, and so do I. Come back when it is done, and not before."

> "Accepted. I have logged the parameters. Do not ask me to adjust them mid-task. The ledger does not appreciate corrections."

### Quest Complete Lines (on quest turn-in)

> "Verified. Tokens awarded. Your contribution to Millbrook's ongoing survival has been recorded and will be forgotten by tomorrow. Such is the nature of public service."

> "Done? Good. Cleanly done? Even better. Your tokens are here. Spend them wisely at the quest shop. Or unwisely. That is between you and your conscience."

> "Another task completed. I will update the ledger. You know, some days I think I do more writing than the adventurers do fighting. The difference is that my work does not involve teeth."

### Farewell Lines (on quest board close)

> "The board updates at dawn. Try to be alive for it."

> "Off you go. And please, if you discover anything noteworthy, report it properly. 'There was a big thing and it was angry' is not a useful description, no matter how many times people try."

## Integration Notes

- **Same dialogue system as N-01 through N-04:** Keyed by NPC ID and event type, random selection. New event types: "quest_accept" and "quest_complete" for quest interactions.
- **Character name (Aldric Voss):** Fifth named Millbrook NPC. Display as nameplate on the quest board UI panel.
- **Cross-references:**
  - Greeting line references Bram Holloway (N-01) and the rat problem, connecting to the vermin bestiary (M-01) and the ongoing rat economy joke.
  - Idle line about the singing lessons pays off the notice board detail from Z-01 ("one very persistent advertisement for singing lessons") and implicates Maren Ashwick (N-03).
  - The rat tails arithmetic line connects to the Rat Tail item (I-04) and the general theme of adventurers as unreliable narrators.
  - "Tokens" and "quest shop" are referenced naturally, introducing the mechanical economy through character voice.
- **Personality niche:** Bram (commerce), Kessa (craft), Maren (community), Vesper (knowledge), Aldric (order). He is the bureaucracy of Millbrook: the person who makes sure that killing monsters translates into a functioning economy. His exasperation is comedic but grounded; someone has to do the paperwork, and he takes it seriously.
- **Quest cadence references:** The greeting line mentions "three new dailies, one weekly," reflecting the actual quest generation system. This grounds the UI in narrative: the board updates because Aldric updates it.
- **The ledger:** A recurring character detail (like Kessa's hammer or Maren's memory) that can be referenced in other NPC dialogue and future quest content.
