# HP_CONSTANTS Analysis

Covers `HP_CONSTANTS` and its interactions with combat knockout/recovery, passive regen, rest healing, flee mechanics, and potions.

## Current Values

| Constant | Value | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| BASE_HP | 100 | flat HP |
| HP_PER_VITALITY | 5 | HP per vitality point |
| BASE_PASSIVE_REGEN | 0.4 | HP/second |
| PASSIVE_REGEN_PER_VITALITY | 0.04 | HP/second per vitality point |
| BASE_REST_HEAL | 2 | HP per turn spent |
| REST_HEAL_PER_VITALITY | 0.2 | HP per turn per vitality point |
| RECOVERY_TURNS_PER_MAX_HP | 1 | multiplier (recovery cost = maxHp * 1) |
| RECOVERY_EXIT_HP_PERCENT | 0.25 | fraction of maxHp on recovery exit |
| LOW_HP_WARNING_THRESHOLD | 0.25 | UI-only threshold |

## Derived Formulas

```
maxHp = 100 + vitality * 5 + equipmentHealthBonus

regenPerSecond = 0.4 + vitality * 0.04

healPerTurn = 2 + vitality * 0.2

recoveryCost = maxHp * 1  (i.e., recoveryCost = maxHp)

recoveryExitHp = floor(maxHp * 0.25)
```

## Scaling Curves

All HP formulas are **strictly linear** in vitality, with no diminishing returns or exponential growth.

### Max HP Scaling (ignoring equipment)

| Vitality | maxHp | % increase over base |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 100 | 0% |
| 10 | 150 | +50% |
| 20 | 200 | +100% |
| 50 | 350 | +250% |
| 99 | 595 | +495% |

Character level caps at 100, yielding 99 attribute points (level 1 grants 0). A player who dumps all 99 points into vitality reaches 595 HP. In practice, players split across 6 attributes (vitality, strength, dexterity, intelligence, luck, evasion), so a typical endgame vitality of ~15-25 is realistic, putting HP at 175-225 before equipment.

### Passive Regen Scaling

| Vitality | regen/sec | seconds to full heal (from 0 to maxHp) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.40 | 250s (4.2 min) |
| 10 | 0.80 | 188s (3.1 min) |
| 20 | 1.20 | 167s (2.8 min) |
| 50 | 2.40 | 146s (2.4 min) |

Full-heal times converge toward ~2.4 min because both maxHp and regen scale linearly with vitality, but maxHp has a large base offset (100) while regen has a small base (0.4). The ratio `maxHp / regenPerSecond` simplifies to `(100 + 5v) / (0.4 + 0.04v)`. As v approaches infinity, this approaches `5/0.04 = 125 seconds`. At v=0, it is `100/0.4 = 250 seconds`.

This is a healthy convergence -- passive regen gets proportionally better with vitality investment, rewarding the stat without breaking the curve.

### Rest Healing Efficiency

Rest spends turns (1 turn/second regen rate) to heal HP. The key question: is resting more efficient than just waiting for passive regen?

| Vitality | healPerTurn | passive regen/sec | passive HP per turn equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 2.0 | 0.40 | 0.40 |
| 10 | 4.0 | 0.80 | 0.80 |
| 20 | 6.0 | 1.20 | 1.20 |
| 50 | 12.0 | 2.40 | 2.40 |

Since turns regen at 1/second and passive HP regen is `0.4 + 0.04v` HP/second, the ratio of rest efficiency to passive regen is:

```
rest_vs_passive = healPerTurn / regenPerSecond
                = (2 + 0.2v) / (0.4 + 0.04v)
```

At v=0: `2/0.4 = 5x`
At v=10: `4/0.8 = 5x`
At v=50: `12/2.4 = 5x`
At v=infinity: `0.2/0.04 = 5x`

**Rest is exactly 5x more efficient than passive regen at every vitality level.** This is because `REST_HEAL_PER_VITALITY / PASSIVE_REGEN_PER_VITALITY = 0.2/0.04 = 5` and `BASE_REST_HEAL / BASE_PASSIVE_REGEN = 2/0.4 = 5`. The constants were clearly designed to maintain this exact ratio. A player always prefers resting over waiting, but resting costs turns that could be spent on combat/exploration.

### Turns to Full Heal via Rest

| Vitality | maxHp | healPerTurn | turns to full heal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 100 | 2.0 | 50 |
| 10 | 150 | 4.0 | 38 |
| 20 | 200 | 6.0 | 34 |
| 50 | 350 | 12.0 | 30 |

Cost: `turns = maxHp / healPerTurn = (100 + 5v) / (2 + 0.2v)`

Limit as v -> inf: `5/0.2 = 25 turns`. So full heals converge to ~25 turns regardless of HP pool size. At v=0 it costs 50 turns. This is well-calibrated -- a full heal from zero never exceeds one combat encounter's turn cost (50 turns per `ENCOUNTER_TURN_COST`).

## Knockout/Recovery Analysis

When a player is knocked out (dies in combat or fails a flee):
1. HP set to 0, `isRecovering = true`
2. Recovery cost = `maxHp * 1` turns
3. Player must spend that many turns to exit recovery
4. On exit, HP = `floor(maxHp * 0.25)`
5. No passive regen while recovering

### Total Turn Cost of Knockout

After recovery exit, the player is at 25% HP and must heal the remaining 75%:

```
recoveryTurns = maxHp
healTurns = ceil(0.75 * maxHp / healPerTurn)
totalKnockoutCost = maxHp + ceil(0.75 * maxHp / (2 + 0.2v))
```

| Vitality | maxHp | recovery turns | heal to full | total knockout cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 100 | 100 | 38 | 138 |
| 10 | 150 | 150 | 29 | 179 |
| 20 | 200 | 200 | 25 | 225 |
| 50 | 350 | 350 | 22 | 372 |

The recovery cost scales linearly with maxHp, meaning vitality investment **increases** the knockout penalty. This is the one area where HP scaling hurts the player -- a high-vitality tank pays a proportionally similar (but absolutely larger) penalty for death.

At v=0, knockout costs 138 turns (2.3 encounters worth at 50+50 turns for encounter+rest).
At v=50, knockout costs 372 turns (~7.4 encounters worth).

### Passive Regen During Offline Recovery

Players are blocked from passive regen while `isRecovering = true`. After paying recovery turns and exiting at 25% HP, passive regen resumes. Time to passively regen from 25% to 100%:

```
passiveRecoveryTime = 0.75 * maxHp / regenPerSecond
```

| Vitality | time from 25% to 100% |
|---|---|
| 0 | 188s (3.1 min) |
| 10 | 141s (2.3 min) |
| 20 | 125s (2.1 min) |
| 50 | 109s (1.8 min) |

Players who go AFK after recovery exit will be full HP within ~2-3 minutes. This means the real penalty of knockout is the recovery turns, not the HP deficit afterward.

## Interaction with Potions

Health potions provide flat or percentage healing during combat:

| Potion | Heal | At v=0 (100 HP) | At v=20 (200 HP) | At v=50 (350 HP) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minor Health | 50 flat | 50% pool | 25% pool | 14% pool |
| Health | 150 flat | 150% (full+) | 75% pool | 43% pool |
| Greater Health | 400 flat | 400% (full+) | 200% (full+) | 114% (full+) |
| Minor Recovery | 25% maxHp | 25 HP | 50 HP | 88 HP |
| Recovery | 50% maxHp | 50 HP | 100 HP | 175 HP |
| Greater Recovery | 100% maxHp | 100 HP | 200 HP | 350 HP |

**Issue:** Flat heal potions scale poorly into late game. A Minor Health Potion heals 50% of a new player's pool but only 14% of a vitality-heavy endgame player's pool. Percentage-based potions scale perfectly by design. The flat potions become effectively useless for high-HP builds, which pushes all endgame players toward percentage potions and makes flat potions pure vendor trash.

## Interaction with Flee Mechanics

Flee outcomes interact with HP via `FLEE_CONSTANTS`:

| Outcome | HP Result | Probability (evasion = mob level) |
|---|---|---|
| Clean escape | `floor(maxHp * 0.15)` (min 1) | 6% (30% flee chance * top 20%) |
| Wounded escape | 1 HP (flat) | 24% (30% flee chance * bottom 80%) |
| Knockout | 0 HP + recovery | 70% |

**Issue:** Wounded escape sets HP to a flat 1, regardless of maxHp. A player with 100 HP and a player with 595 HP both end up at 1 HP on wounded escape. For a high-HP player, this means needing ~175 rest turns (at v=99) to get back to full vs ~50 rest turns for a new player. The flat 1 HP is disproportionately punishing as a fraction of maxHp at low vitality (1% of 100 HP) vs high vitality (0.17% of 595 HP), but the actual recovery cost in turns is higher for high-vitality players.

Meanwhile, clean escape gives 15% of maxHp, which scales properly. The inconsistency between the two escape tiers is a minor design smell.

## Issues Found

### 1. Recovery Cost Scales Punitively with Vitality (Medium)

`recoveryCost = maxHp * 1` means a high-vitality player pays absolutely more turns for knockout. At v=0 the cost is 100 turns; at v=50 it is 350 turns. Since vitality doesn't help avoid knockout (that's evasion's job), this creates a hidden tax on HP investment. Players who invest in vitality for survivability are punished more harshly when they do die.

The counterargument: high-HP players die less often, so the higher per-death cost is offset by lower death frequency. But this depends on encounter design, and it creates a feel-bad moment when it does happen.

### 2. Flat Heal Potions Obsolete at High HP (Low)

Minor Health Potion (50 flat) becomes trivial for any player with >200 HP. This is a standard RPG tiering issue and not critical -- players simply upgrade to percentage potions. But if flat potions are intended to remain useful, they need HP-scaling.

### 3. Wounded Escape Flat 1 HP Inconsistency (Low)

`PARTIAL_SUCCESS_HP = 1` is the only flee outcome that doesn't scale with maxHp. Clean escape gives 15% maxHp, knockout gives 0 + recovery cost. Wounded escape giving flat 1 HP creates an odd valley where the "better" outcome (not knocked out) can feel nearly as bad as knockout for high-HP players, since they need many rest turns to recover from 1 HP.

### 4. No Diminishing Returns on Vitality (Information Only)

Vitality provides perfectly linear HP and regen scaling forever. With 6 attributes competing for ~99 points, natural competition prevents this from being an issue. But if future systems add more attribute points (achievements already can via `attributePoints: { increment: reward.amount }`), the linear curve could become problematic.

## Recommendations

### 1. Consider a Sub-Linear Recovery Cost

Instead of `recoveryCost = maxHp`, consider:
```
recoveryCost = BASE_RECOVERY_COST + maxHp * RECOVERY_TURNS_PER_MAX_HP
```
With `BASE_RECOVERY_COST = 50` and reducing `RECOVERY_TURNS_PER_MAX_HP` from 1.0 to 0.5:

| Vitality | maxHp | Current cost | Proposed cost | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 0% |
| 10 | 150 | 150 | 125 | -17% |
| 20 | 200 | 200 | 150 | -25% |
| 50 | 350 | 350 | 225 | -36% |

This smooths the penalty curve while keeping death meaningful. Low-level players are unaffected.

**Risk:** Low. Only reduces late-game death penalty, which is already the most punishing part of the system.

### 2. Make Wounded Escape Percentage-Based

Change `PARTIAL_SUCCESS_HP = 1` to a percentage like `PARTIAL_SUCCESS_HP_PERCENT = 0.05` (5% maxHp). This keeps wounded escape worse than clean escape (5% vs 15%) while scaling properly.

| Vitality | Current (1 HP) | Proposed (5% maxHp) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 / 100 (1%) | 5 / 100 (5%) |
| 10 | 1 / 150 (0.7%) | 8 / 150 (5%) |
| 50 | 1 / 350 (0.3%) | 18 / 350 (5%) |

**Risk:** Low. Requires a code change in `fleeMechanics.ts` (change from flat value to `Math.max(1, Math.floor(maxHp * percent))`), but the change is contained in one function.

### 3. No Change Needed on Flat Potions

Flat potions filling a "cheap early-game consumable" role is fine. Percentage potions exist for late game. This is standard RPG tiering.

## Risk Level

**low** -- The HP system is well-calibrated with a clean 5x rest-vs-passive ratio and healthy convergence curves. The recovery cost scaling and flat wounded-escape HP are minor rough edges, not exploitable degenerate strategies. No player can abuse the system to gain an unfair advantage; the issues are about proportional fairness of punishment across different vitality levels.
