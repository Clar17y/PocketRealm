## Analysis

### 1. Guild XP Leveling Curve

Formula: `XP_needed(level) = floor(100 * level^1.8)`

| Guild Level | XP Needed | Cumulative XP | Mob Kills to Level | Days at 500 kills/day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 100 | 100 | 100 | <1 |
| 5 | 1,637 | 4,963 | 1,637 | 3.3 |
| 10 | 6,310 | 25,700 | 6,310 | 12.6 |
| 15 | 13,572 | 72,410 | 13,572 | 27.1 |
| 20 | 23,452 | 152,840 | 23,452 | 46.9 |
| 25 | 35,938 | 276,100 | 35,938 | 71.9 |
| 30 | 51,016 | 450,580 | 51,016 | 102.0 |
| 40 | 88,586 | 949,890 | 88,586 | 177.2 |

The curve is sub-quadratic (exponent 1.8). This is a reasonable middle ground -- faster than quadratic but significantly harder than linear. A guild with 10 active members each killing 500 mobs/day generates ~5,000 guild XP/day from combat alone. Adding crafts, boss rounds, and contracts, a healthy guild might produce 8,000-12,000 XP/day.

At that rate:
- Level 10: ~3-4 days (cumulative 25,700 XP) -- reasonable for spec unlock
- Level 25: ~25-35 days -- reasonable for Tier 2 upgrades/specs
- Level 40: ~80-120 days -- late-game guild identity milestone

This feels well-paced. The exponent 1.8 prevents exponential runaway while still creating meaningful long-term goals.

### 2. Member Capacity Scaling

Formula: `max_members = 10 + floor(level / 2)`

| Guild Level | Max Members | Members per Level |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 | -- |
| 10 | 15 | 0.5/level |
| 20 | 20 | 0.5/level |
| 30 | 25 | 0.5/level |
| 40 | 30 | 0.5/level |

This is purely linear at 0.5 members per guild level. The slow scaling means guilds stay relatively small (max ~30 at endgame), which keeps the community intimate but also limits guild XP generation.

### 3. Treasury Economy: Income vs. Spending

**Treasury capacity:** `100,000 + level * 10,000`

| Guild Level | Treasury Cap |
|---|---|
| 1 | 110,000 |
| 10 | 200,000 |
| 25 | 350,000 |
| 40 | 500,000 |

**Income sources:**
- Tax on member turn spending (0-20%)
- Contract completion rewards (500-2,000 turns per contract, 3/week)

**Tax income modeling** (at 20% max rate):
A player regenerates 86,400 turns/day. Assuming a player spends ~40,000 turns/day on activities (exploration, combat, crafting, gathering):
- Tax per player per day: `40,000 * 0.20 = 8,000 turns`
- 10-member guild: ~80,000 turns/day
- 20-member guild: ~160,000 turns/day

**Contract income per week:** 3 contracts * avg 1,250 = 3,750 treasury turns/week = ~536/day.

Tax dominates contract income by ~150:1. Contracts are essentially irrelevant as a treasury income source.

**Spending:**
- Tier 1 upgrades: 5,000-8,000 per 2 hours = 60,000-96,000/day if maintained 24/7
- Tier 3 upgrades: 20,000-25,000 per 2 hours = 240,000-300,000/day if maintained 24/7
- Level 1 projects: 500,000 one-time
- Level 3 projects: 5,000,000 one-time
- Spec respec: 2,000,000 one-time

**Upgrade sustainability analysis (all 5 upgrades running 24/7):**
- Tier 1 (all 5): `(5,000 + 5,000 + 8,000 + 8,000 + 5,000) * 12 = 372,000 turns/day`
- Tier 3 (all 5): `(20,000 + 20,000 + 25,000 + 25,000 + 20,000) * 12 = 1,320,000 turns/day`

A 10-member guild at 20% tax generates ~80,000/day. This cannot even sustain all five Tier 1 upgrades simultaneously. The guild must choose 1-2 upgrades to keep active.

A 20-member guild at 20% tax generates ~160,000/day -- still under the 372,000 needed for all Tier 1 upgrades.

### 4. Treasury Cap as a Bottleneck

At level 25, treasury cap = 350,000. But Level 2 projects cost 2,000,000 and Level 3 projects cost 5,000,000. **The treasury cap is far below project costs.**

Wait -- re-reading the code: `guildService.ts` line 58 checks `guild.treasuryTurns < def.treasuryCost` but the cap is only enforced during tax deposits (line 111 in `guildTaxService.ts`). The treasury can grow **beyond the cap** through contract rewards, which bypass the cap check. But contract rewards max at 2,000 turns -- trivially small.

**This is a critical issue.** The treasury cap at level 25 is 350,000 but Level 2 projects require 1,500,000-2,000,000 treasury turns. Even at level 40, the cap is 500,000. There appears to be no way to accumulate enough treasury to start Level 2 or Level 3 projects.

Looking more carefully: contract completion in `guildContractService.ts` line 155 directly increments `treasuryTurns` without a cap check. But avg ~1,250/contract is far too little to bridge the gap.

**Possible resolution:** The treasury cap may be intended only to limit passive tax accumulation, while a guild leader might have a direct "contribute turns" mechanism. But reading the code, I see no such route. The only treasury inputs are:
1. Tax deposits (capped)
2. Contract rewards (uncapped but tiny: ~3,750/week)

At 3,750 turns/week uncapped from contracts, reaching 2,000,000 for a Level 2 project would take **533 weeks (10+ years)**. This is clearly broken.

**Resolution hypothesis:** The treasury cap might be a soft cap on the tax-deposit path only, and the actual treasury can exceed it. Looking at the tax service: `actualTax = Math.min(taxAmount, cap - membership.guild.treasuryTurns)`. Once treasury hits cap, tax stops flowing. Contract rewards still flow in uncapped. But at 3,750/week from contracts, reaching project costs is infeasible.

### 5. Boost Scaling and Active Player Thresholds

The boost scaling system: <5 active = 50%, 5-9 = 75%, 10+ = 100%.

"Active" means any XP activity within 48 hours. The 48-hour window is generous -- even casual players who log in every other day qualify.

The breakpoints create a cliff: 4 active members get 50% effectiveness, 5 get 75%. This is a 50% jump in value for adding one member. Similarly, going from 9 to 10 is a 33% jump. These cliffs incentivize guilds to recruit exactly to threshold boundaries.

Small guilds (2-4 members) get harsh 50% penalties. This makes upgrades cost-ineffective for small groups:
- 2-player guild, Tier 1 XP boost: `0.05 * 0.5 = 2.5%` XP for 5,000 treasury / 2 hours
- 10-player guild, Tier 1 XP boost: `0.05 * 1.0 = 5.0%` XP for 5,000 treasury / 2 hours

Same cost, half the benefit. Small guilds are doubly punished: less tax income AND less upgrade effectiveness.

### 6. Maximum Bonus Stacking

A guild with all systems maxed can stack:

**Warfare specialization (Tier 3, guild level 40):** +15% XP, +15% combat damage, +5% defense
**Plus Tier 3 upgrades:** +15% XP (2h), +15% combat damage (2h), +15% defense (2h)
**Plus Raid Hall project:** +15% XP (permanent)

Total XP boost: 15% (spec) + 15% (upgrade) + 15% (project) = **45% XP boost**
Total combat damage: 15% (spec) + 15% (upgrade) = **30% combat damage**
Total defense: 5% (spec) + 15% (upgrade) = **20% defense**

For Industry spec:
Crafting crit: 15% (spec) + 15% (upgrade) + 15% (Master Workshop project) = **45% crafting crit**
Gathering yield: 30% (spec) + 30% (upgrade) + 15% (Explorer's Guild project) = **75% gathering yield**

The **75% gathering yield** is very high. Combined with individual skill bonuses, this could make guild gatherers dramatically more efficient than non-guild players.

### 7. Project Turn Contribution Caps

`PER_PROJECT_TURN_CAP = 10,000` per player. Level 1 projects need 100,000 turns.
- Minimum players needed: 100,000 / 10,000 = **10 players**
- Level 2 projects (400,000 turns): 40 players minimum

But max guild size at level 10 is 15 members. Level 2 projects need 400,000 turns with max 15 contributing 10,000 each = 150,000 turns. **Level 2 projects are impossible to complete through turn contributions alone** with a level 10 guild (15 max members * 10,000 cap = 150,000 < 400,000).

At guild level 20 (max 20 members): 200,000 < 400,000. Still impossible.
At guild level 40 (max 30 members): 300,000 < 400,000. Still impossible.

**Level 2 and Level 3 projects cannot be completed** within the turn contribution cap. 400,000 turns requires 40 contributors, but max guild size caps at 30.

**Level 3 projects (1,000,000 turns):** Would need 100 contributors. Completely impossible.

### 8. Contract Target Scaling

Contracts use guild level brackets: low (1-10), mid (11-25), high (26+).

| Contract | Low | Mid | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mob Slayer | 2,000 | 5,000 | 15,000 |
| Family Hunter | 5,000 | 15,000 | 40,000 |
| Master Crafter | 5,000 | 20,000 | 50,000 |
| Rare Artisan | 50 | 150 | 500 |
| Resource Gatherer | 10,000 | 40,000 | 100,000 |
| Pathfinder | 500,000 | 2,000,000 | 5,000,000 |
| Arena Champion | 1,000 | 3,000 | 10,000 |
| Boss Challenger | 500 | 1,500 | 3,750 |

The **Pathfinder** contract at high bracket requires 5,000,000 exploration turns in a single week. At 86,400 turns/day per player, that's 57.9 player-days of pure exploration. Even with 30 members all exploring 100%, that's ~2 days of full turn dumps. Very demanding but theoretically possible.

**Arena Champion** at high bracket (10,000 PvP wins/week) requires ~1,430 wins/day across the guild. At 30 members, that's 48 wins/person/day. Given PvP has cooldowns and matchmaking, this likely requires the entire guild to do nothing but PvP. Borderline impossible.

Contract rewards (200-800 XP, 500-2,000 treasury) are small compared to the effort required at high brackets.

### 9. Guild Creation Cost and Access

50,000 turns at 1 turn/second = 13.9 hours of turn regeneration. A player with full bank (64,800) can create a guild immediately, but it burns 77% of their banked turns. At level 20 (the creation requirement), players have had significant time to build up, so this is a meaningful but not prohibitive cost.

The turn cost is a one-time sunk cost. Since turns regenerate passively, the real cost is ~14 hours of idling. This is appropriately gated -- enough to prevent frivolous guild creation, but not enough to be exclusionary.

### 10. Specialization Respec Cost

2,000,000 treasury turns for respec. As analyzed in section 4, reaching 2,000,000 in the treasury is effectively impossible given the treasury cap (max 500,000 at level 40). Respeccing is broken unless there's a treasury income mechanism that bypasses the cap that I'm not seeing.

## Issues Found

### Critical: Treasury Cap Blocks Projects and Respec (Severity: HIGH)

The treasury cap formula `100,000 + level * 10,000` maxes at 500,000 (level 40), but:
- Level 2 projects cost 1,500,000-2,000,000
- Level 3 projects cost 5,000,000
- Specialization respec costs 2,000,000

Tax income stops when treasury hits cap. Contract rewards (uncapped) average only 3,750/week. There is no feasible path to accumulate enough treasury for any cost exceeding the cap. **Level 2+ projects and spec respec are effectively inaccessible.**

### Critical: Turn Contribution Caps Block L2/L3 Projects (Severity: HIGH)

`PER_PROJECT_TURN_CAP` of 10,000 per player combined with max guild sizes means:
- L2 projects (400,000 turns): need 40 contributors, max guild size is 20-30
- L3 projects (1,000,000 turns): need 100 contributors, max guild size is 30

These projects are mathematically impossible to complete through turn contributions.

### Medium: Contract Rewards Are Negligible

Contract treasury rewards (avg 1,250 per contract, ~3,750/week) are trivial compared to tax income (~80,000-160,000/day at 20% with 10-20 members). Contracts provide <1% of treasury income. The XP rewards (avg 500/contract) are similarly dwarfed by daily mob kill XP (5,000+/day for 10 active members).

### Medium: Boost Scaling Cliffs at 5 and 10 Members

The 50% -> 75% jump at exactly 5 members and 75% -> 100% jump at exactly 10 members create sharp cliff effects. Guilds at 4 or 9 active members experience disproportionate pressure to recruit one more.

### Low: Discovery Spec Gathering Yield May Over-Stack

Discovery Tier 3 grants +50% gathering yield. Combined with upgrade (+30%) and Explorer's Guild project (+15%), total guild gathering yield bonus reaches 95%. If individual skill bonuses stack additively on top, this could create a resource glut.

### Low: XP_PER_BOSS_ROUND Appears Unused

`XP_PER_BOSS_ROUND = 10` is defined but I found no call site in the API code where it is used. It is only referenced in plan documentation. This XP source may never have been implemented.

## Recommendations

### 1. Fix Treasury Cap (Critical)

Raise the treasury cap formula significantly:

**Option A (simple):** `TREASURY_BASE_CAP = 1,000,000`, `TREASURY_CAP_PER_LEVEL = 200,000`
- Level 1: 1,200,000 (enough for L1 projects)
- Level 10: 3,000,000 (enough for L2 projects + respec)
- Level 25: 6,000,000 (enough for L3 projects)
- Level 40: 9,000,000

**Option B (exponential):** `cap = 100,000 * level^1.5`
- Level 10: 3,162,277
- Level 25: 12,500,000
- Level 40: 25,298,221

Option A is simpler and still provides clear progression gates.

### 2. Fix Turn Contribution Caps (Critical)

Either raise `PER_PROJECT_TURN_CAP` or lower project turn goals:

**Option A:** Raise cap to `PER_PROJECT_TURN_CAP = 50,000`
- L2 projects (400k): 8 contributors needed (feasible at 15-member guild)
- L3 projects (1M): 20 contributors needed (feasible at 25-30 member guild)

**Option B:** Lower project turn goals
- L2: 400,000 -> 150,000 (15 contributors at 10k cap)
- L3: 1,000,000 -> 300,000 (30 contributors at 10k cap)

Option A is preferable because it makes larger individual contributions meaningful while keeping the collaborative requirement.

### 3. Increase Contract Rewards

Scale contract rewards with guild level bracket to make them meaningful:

```
REWARD_TREASURY_MIN: { low: 5,000, mid: 15,000, high: 50,000 }
REWARD_TREASURY_MAX: { low: 15,000, mid: 50,000, high: 150,000 }
```

This makes contracts a secondary but non-trivial treasury source (~10-15% of tax income).

### 4. Smooth Boost Scaling

Replace the 3-tier step function with a linear ramp:

```
scale = min(1.0, 0.3 + 0.07 * activeCount)
```

- 1 active: 37%
- 5 active: 65%
- 10 active: 100%
- No cliffs, monotonically increasing

### 5. Implement XP_PER_BOSS_ROUND

Wire `addGuildXp(guildId, GUILD_CONSTANTS.XP_PER_BOSS_ROUND)` into the boss round resolution service if it is not already connected. This was part of the design but appears unimplemented.

## Risk Level

**high** -- Two critical issues (treasury cap and turn contribution caps) make the entire L2/L3 project system and spec respec inaccessible. These are mathematically broken: no amount of player effort can overcome the caps. The guild endgame content is effectively unreachable without constant changes.
